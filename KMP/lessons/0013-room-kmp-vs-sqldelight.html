Room KMP vs. SQLDelight: Comparison & Trade-Offs

Analyze the technical trade-offs between Google's official Jetpack Room KMP and Cash App's SQLDelight.

1. The Why & Architectural Mental Model

Choosing a database persistence engine is one of the highest-leverage architectural decisions in KMP. Currently, two major players dominate:

Criteria Google Jetpack Room KMP Cash App SQLDelight
Philosophy Annotation-driven ORM (@Entity, @Dao) SQL-first compiler (.sq files → Kotlin types)
Kotlin 2.x Support Full KSP2 compiler support Full compiler plugin support
Best For Android teams migrating to KMP; CRUD schemas Complex custom SQL queries, high-throughput financial apps

3. Production Pitfalls & Anti-Patterns

Pitfall: Android SQLiteDriver on iOS Targets
Room KMP requires BundledSQLiteDriver on iOS/Native targets. Attempting to use the platform Android SQLite framework driver on Apple devices will fail to link. Always configure the driver via expect/actual or DI.
