# Dating & Relationship Mastery Glossary

Canonical vocabulary for this workspace across all 28 modules (`0001`–`0025`, `OPT-01`–`OPT-03`) and printable sheets ([REF-01](./reference/dating-funnel-reference.html), [REF-02](./reference/venue-opener-playbook.html), [REF-03](./reference/global-cultural-matrix.html)).

## Phase I–II: Calibration & Sourcing

- **Dating Funnel (Pipeline):** The sequential path from personal baseline (`0`) → sourcing (`1`) → approach (`2`) → conversation (`3`) → contact close (`4`) → texting & dates (`5`) → physicality & intimacy (`6`) → relationship & maintenance (`7`). Diagnosing drop-offs isolates which link to fix.
- **Warmth × Competence:** Science of People framework showing first impressions require *warmth* (visible hands, relaxed shoulders, genuine smile, safety) before *competence* (confidence, status, direction).
- **Non-Neediness:** Mark Manson's baseline principle: caring more about honest self-expression and mutual compatibility than chasing approval from strangers.
- **Micro-Leak:** Subtle mismatch between words and tense body language (forced bravado, rushed speech, hidden hands) that triggers distrust.
- **Third Place:** Recurring daytime or community venue outside home and work (bookstores, specialty cafes, volunteer sites, hobby clubs, church socials).
- **Open vs. Closed Window:** Situational cues signaling approachability (browsing casually, pausing, packing up) vs. closed focus (noise-canceling headphones, rushing, deep laptop deadlines).

## Phase III: Approach, Conversation & Closing

- **Systematic Desensitization Ladder:** Nick Notas's progressive 4-step exposure progression (`Level 1` service greetings → `Level 2` hit-and-run compliments → `Level 3` brief situational questions → `Level 4` full honest approaches).
- **3-Second Rule:** Stepping in within 3 seconds of noticing an open window before anxious inner critique builds and hovering looks awkward.
- **Observation + Assumption Hook:** Opening or transitioning with a playful, low-pressure observation and guess rather than dry "interview" questions.
- **90-Second Hook Check:** Evaluating whether a stranger is turning toward your bid (torso/feet angled in, follow-up questions, warmth) vs. offering polite tolerance (one-word answers, angled away).
- **Threading / Follow-Up Loop:** Anchoring your next remark or question on the emotional core of what she just said rather than jumping topics.
- **Seeding:** Mentioning a fun shared spot, food, or activity naturally mid-conversation before asking for a number so the close feels effortless.
- **Instant Date:** Converting a lively daytime or campus conversation directly into a 15–30 minute spontaneous coffee, walk, or snack nearby.

## Phase IV–V: Texting, Dates & Physicality

- **Callback Text:** Short same-day or next-morning message referencing a specific inside joke or moment from meeting in person.
- **Two-Stop Date:** Structuring Date 1 across two nearby venues (e.g., cozy tea/drink with 90-degree corner seating → short scenic walk to dessert) to build familiarity and shared memory.
- **Mutual Qualification:** Screening compatibility, kindness, accountability, and shared values on Dates 1–3+ rather than auditioning blindly.
- **Dual Control Model (Accelerators & Brakes):** Dr. Emily Nagoski's sexual science model: Sexual Excitation responds to turn-ons while Sexual Inhibition ("brakes"—stress, rush, insecurity, unsafe logistics) blocks arousal until eased.
- **Responsive Desire:** Desire that emerges *after* emotional safety, warmth, and unhurried foreplay begin rather than spontaneously beforehand.
- **Physical Escalation Ladder:** Calibrated 5-rung touch progression (warm greeting → incidental arm/shoulder touch → close proximity → hand-holding → triangle-gaze kiss) checking reciprocation at every rung.
- **Aftercare:** Warm cuddling, water/coffee, affection, and next-day reassurance after physical intimacy.

## Phase VI: Attachment & Relationship Maintenance

- **Attachment Styles (`Secure`, `Anxious`, `Avoidant`):** Levine & Heller's adult bonding patterns; includes **protest behaviors** (guilt-trips, rapid follow-up texts) and **deactivating strategies** (pulling away when intimacy deepens).
- **Earned Security:** Deliberately building secure communication habits and choosing secure partners regardless of earlier anxious/avoidant tendencies.
- **Bid for Connection & Turning Toward:** Gottman's core metric—everyday small invitations to connect; happy long-term couples turn toward ~86% of bids vs. ~33% in distressed couples.
- **Soft Startup & Repair Attempt:** Raising complaints using `"I feel ... about [moment] ... and need ..."` instead of `"You always..."`, and de-escalating physiological flooding early.

## Phase VII & Optional Track: Cross-Cultural Vocabulary

- **High-Context vs. Low-Context (`Culture Map`):** Erin Meyer's scale measuring whether meaning is stated explicitly (USA, Germany, Nordics) or implied indirectly to protect harmony and face (East & Southeast Asia).
- **Relationship-Based vs. Task-Based Trust:** Whether trust is built privately 1-on-1 first or through shared meals, social circles, and family integration early.
- **Kuuki wo yomu / Chotto muzukashii / Kokuhaku (Japan):** Reading the air; indirect polite refusal (*"a bit difficult"*); explicit verbal confession transitioning dating into an official couple.
- **Ilk-ssip / Cha (South Korea):** Leaving KakaoTalk messages read-and-ignored (`1` badge gone); structured multi-round date flow (dinner → cafe/drinks).
- **Pengyouquan (China):** WeChat Moments feed used as key lifestyle/character social proof.
- **Sanuk / Jeeb / Kreng Jai / Jai Yen (Thailand):** Lighthearted fun; sweet steady courting; reluctance to impose or say blunt no; keeping a cool, calm heart in public.
- **Imieniny / Pantofle (Poland & Belarus):** Cherished Name Days; indoor slippers offered when removing outdoor shoes (alongside odd-numbered flower gifting).
- **Personalismo / Caballerosidad / Familismo / Noviazgo (Latin America):** Warm expressive rapport; attentive chivalry; family centrality; defined committed courtship.
