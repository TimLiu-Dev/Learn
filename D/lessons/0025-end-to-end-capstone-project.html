1. Project Goal & End-to-End Architecture

Knowledge without real-world execution produces only fluency, not durable 2027 capability. This capstone bridges theory into real results across a structured 6-Week Field Sprint covering all 8 pipeline stages plus cross-cultural scenario mastery.

Every sprint delivers measurable, controllable outputs rather than waiting passively on luck.

Capstone Passing Bar Target 80%+ on the 10-question comprehensive exam below AND 75/100 points on the Field Execution Rubric.

2. Stage Deliverables (Weeks 1–6 Execution)

  • Deliverable A — Baseline & Logistics Ready (Week 1): Two tailored daily outfits, spotless home hospitality sweep, 3 scouted 2-stop date routes, and updated 6-photo hybrid profile.
  • Deliverable B — Multi-Channel Sourcing (Weeks 1–6): Participate actively across at least 3 distinct venue categories from Phase II (e.g., bookstores/cafes, church/volunteering, school/campus or clubs, social mixers).
  • Deliverable C — 20 Calibrated Approaches & Hooks (Weeks 2–5): Log 20 warm 3-second entries (mixing Level 2–4 reps), tracking hook signals (green vs. polite exit) without lingering past closed cues.
  • Deliverable D — Contact Close & Texting Conversion (Weeks 2–6): Convert conversations into contacts using seeded context, send same-day callbacks, and book at least 3+ Dates (executing two-stop Date 1 + interactive Date 2/3).
  • Deliverable E — Escalation, Consent & Attachment Debrief (Weeks 4–6): Reflect cleanly on physical ladder calibration, boundary respect, and Green/Red Flag + Attachment Style vetting for every person dated.
  • Deliverable F — Cross-Cultural Calibration Case Study: Draft one full cultural adaptation plan for your top 2027 international target (Poland/Belarus, China/Taiwan/Korea/Japan, Vietnam/Thailand, or Western Europe/Latina track).
Integrity Standard A 'success' in the log includes clean, respectful exits on low-interest signals—never pushing past boundaries.
