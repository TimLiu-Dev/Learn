# Dating & Relationship Mastery Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Models: Attract Women Through Honesty_ — Mark Manson](https://markmanson.net/books/models)
  Foundational framework on non-neediness, honest intent, polarization, and lifestyle alignment. Use for: attraction fundamentals, approaching without approval-seeking, and filtering compatibility.
- [Research Hub: The Gottman Institute — Drs. John & Julie Gottman](https://www.gottman.com/)
  Longitudinal empirical research on emotional bids ("turning toward" vs. "turning away/against") and relationship stability. Use for: conversation responsiveness, conflict resolution, and long-term relationship maintenance.
- [Behavioral Lab: Science of People — Vanessa Van Edwards](https://www.scienceofpeople.com/)
  Research-backed communication, body language cues (warmth vs. competence, visible hands), and deep follow-up question frameworks. Use for: first impressions, body language calibration, and moving past small talk.
- [Practical Field Guides — Nick Notas](https://nicknotas.com/)
  Action-oriented, systematic desensitization and anti-overthinking dating mechanics. Use for: overcoming approach anxiety, daytime/social-venue openers, and simple logistics.
- [Book: _Not Nice_ — Dr. Aziz Gazipura](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Dr+Aziz+Gazipura+Not+Nice)
  Clinical psychology perspective on breaking the approval trap, people-pleasing, and social fear. Use for: inner confidence, handling rejection without shame, and setting boundaries.
- [Book: _Attached_ — Dr. Amir Levine & Rachel Heller](https://www.attachedthebook.com/)
  Adult attachment theory (Secure, Anxious, Avoidant) and the anxious-avoidant trap. Use for: texting anxiety calibration, partner vetting, and building earned security.
- [Framework: _The Culture Map_ — Erin Meyer](https://erinmeyer.com/)
  Cross-cultural communication dimensions (high-context vs. low-context, direct vs. indirect feedback, task-based vs. relationship-based trust). Use for: navigating dating norms across Europe, North America, East Asia, and Southeast Asia.
- [Book: _Come As You Are_ — Dr. Emily Nagoski](https://www.emilynagoski.com/)
  Evidence-based sexual science: Dual Control Model (Sexual Excitation vs. Sexual Inhibition / "accelerators and brakes"), responsive vs. spontaneous desire, and enthusiastic consent communication. Use for: Phase V intimacy, removing anxiety/brakes, and sexual communication.
- [Cross-Cultural Etiquette Guides — Poland, Belarus, East & Southeast Asia, Latin America](https://erinmeyer.com/)
  Verified cultural mechanics compiled from regional dating & etiquette research: Polish/Belarusian chivalry and odd-flower customs; Japanese *kuuki wo yomu* & *kokuhaku*; Korean KakaoTalk *ilk-ssip* norms; Chinese/Taiwanese WeChat *Pengyouquan* & action-based care; Thai *sanuk*/*kreng jai*/*jai yen*; Vietnamese family care/curfews; Latin American *familismo*, *personalismo*, and *caballerosidad*. Use for: Phase VII and Optional Track (`OPT-01`–`OPT-03`).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Offline: Local recurring third-place communities (volunteer organizations, church young-adult groups, book clubs, language exchanges, partner dance/fitness classes)
  Use for: practicing warm-network social skills, building natural social proof, and meeting compatible people outside high-noise nightlife.
- Online: Moderated self-improvement and communication communities (e.g., Gottman blog discussions, language/cultural exchange meetups for international calibration)
  Use for: sanity-checking cultural etiquette and communication blind spots.
