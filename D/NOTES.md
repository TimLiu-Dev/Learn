# Learning Notes & Preferences

## User Preferences
- **Goal**: End-to-end dating mastery to find a girlfriend, date, and connect with partners of choice with a high probability of success in 2027.
- **Format**:
  1. `master-plan.html` created first as the comprehensive end-to-end legend for all lessons.
  2. Lesson `0001` built as the structural, visual, and pedagogical benchmark for all subsequent lessons.
  3. Multiple-choice quizzes in every lesson with **full explanations for both why the correct answer is right AND why every incorrect option is wrong**.
  4. Equal word count / no formatting clues across MCQ options (per `teach` skill).
  5. Comprehensive end-to-end Capstone Project at the end of the curriculum.
- **Topics Requested (Must All Be Covered)**:
  - Sourcing/venues: bookstores, schools/colleges (campuses, classes, student clubs), church events, volunteering, professional networking events &amp; conferences, everyday venues, nightlife, online/hybrid.
  - Core pipeline: approach, talking/pickup, phone number close, texting, 1st/2nd/3rd+ dates, sex/intimacy, transitioning to a relationship.
  - Cross-cultural / foreign & background dating:
    - Countries/regions: China, Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Vietnam, Thailand, Poland, Belarus, wider Europe, and America.
    - Optional Cultural & Background Section (`OPT-01` to `OPT-03`): dating across **Asian**, **White** (North American & European), and **Latina** backgrounds (both domestic/diaspora and international contexts), focusing on real communication, family, chivalry, and pacing norms without reducing individuals to stereotypes.
