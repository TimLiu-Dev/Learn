How to Read This Legend

Most dating advice fails because it isolates one tactic—a clever opening line or a texting trick—while ignoring the rest of the pipeline. Getting a phone number means nothing if your texting loses momentum; great first dates stall if you misread physical escalation; and domestic habits break down when dating across high-context cultures in East Asia or traditional courtship norms in Eastern Europe.

This workspace treats dating as an end-to-end calibrated skill stack built on three pillars: Knowledge (evidence-backed psychology and cultural frameworks), Skills (realistic scenario quizzes, multi-turn dialogue scripts, and progressive field reps), and Wisdom (real-world iteration across varied communities). Check off each module above as you finish its quiz and micro-rep; progress saves locally in your browser.

How Every Lesson Is Structured (Benchmark Standard)

Every module in ./lessons/ follows the template established in Lesson 0001:

  • Tight Scope (6–9 min): One core skill per file so your working memory stays sharp.
  • Cited Frameworks & Examples: Grounded in primary sources in RESOURCES.md, canonical terms in GLOSSARY.md, sample dialogues, and diagrams.
  • Scenario MCQ Quiz: Equal-length, realistic options with randomized answer positions and full explanations for why the right option works AND why each distractor fails.
  • Real-World Micro-Rep: A concrete 15–30 minute field exercise to turn knowledge into real-world habit.
