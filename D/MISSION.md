# Mission: End-to-End Dating & Relationship Mastery (2027 Target)

## Why
Build complete, reliable, real-world capability across the entire dating lifecycle—from meeting women in everyday venues, communities, and international settings to building attraction, dating, physical intimacy, and choosing a compatible girlfriend—so the user enters 2027 able to attract and connect with partners of choice with high confidence and consistent results.

## Success looks like
- Consistently spot opportunities and initiate warm, calibrated conversations in bookstores, schools/colleges, church events, volunteering, professional networking events, social venues, and daily life without freezing.
- Convert initial conversations into phone numbers/contacts, engaging text threads, and well-paced 1st, 2nd, and 3rd+ dates.
- Navigate physical escalation, sexual intimacy, boundaries, and respectful consent smoothly and authentically.
- Vet compatibility using attachment and communication frameworks, transitioning smoothly into a healthy, committed relationship.
- Adapt approach, texting, dating pace, and expectations across cultures and backgrounds (including North America, Western/Eastern Europe such as Poland & Belarus, East & Southeast Asia such as China, Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Vietnam & Thailand, plus an optional cultural/background track covering Asian, White, and Latina dating dynamics domestically and abroad).
- Complete the end-to-end Capstone Field Project synthesizing every stage from sourcing to relationship evaluation.

## Constraints
- Lessons must be self-contained HTML files in `./lessons/` backed by shared components in `./assets/` and a central `./master-plan.html` legend.
- Every lesson must stay concise and focused on one tangible win, include multiple-choice quizzes with equal-length options and full explanations for why both the right answer is correct and every wrong option is incorrect, and recommend a primary source.
- Learning progresses at a sustainable pace from foundational calibration toward 2027 real-world execution.

## Out of scope
- Manipulative pickup-artist (PUA) scripts, deceptive tactics, or ignoring clear rejection/consent boundaries.
- Treating cross-cultural guidelines as rigid stereotypes rather than baseline cultural tendencies to calibrate against individuals.
