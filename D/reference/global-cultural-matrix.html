2. Universal Cross-Cultural & Travel Safeguards

  • Verify language comprehension gently: Speak clearly without idioms or rapid sarcasm when English is a second language; never mistake language hesitation for lack of intelligence or interest.
  • Never assume uniform stereotypes: A second-generation Asian-American or Latina raised in Chicago may have very different expectations from someone raised in Taipei, Warsaw, or Medellín—ask open questions and observe her cues.
  • Protect face in high-context cultures: Avoid blunt public criticism, teasing that mocks someone's insecurities, or forcing an explicit verbal rejection when she gives a soft scheduling excuse.
  • Travel & scam hygiene abroad: Meet only in reputable public spots you choose on Date 1, never follow strangers to unfamiliar "VIP" clubs with unpriced menus, and treat early money/crypto/visa requests as hard red flags.
