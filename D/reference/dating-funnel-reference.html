The Dating Funnel & Calibration Matrix

Compressed field reference for Lesson 0001: diagnose where interactions drop off and calibrate warmth, intent, and receptivity in real time.

1. The 8-Stage Funnel Diagnostic

Stage Core Objective Primary Failure Symptom Primary Fix
0. Baseline Grooming, calm nervous system, logistics Freeze up or apologize for existing Visible hands, steady posture, desensitization reps
1. Sourcing Be where compatible people gather Zero new weekly introductions Mix bookstores, church, volunteering, clubs, apps
2. Approach Enter cleanly within 3 seconds Hesitate until opportunity passes Simple observational/direct opener; warmth first
3. Conversation Spark rapport & emotional polarity Polite "interview" fizzles out fast Follow-up loops, playful banter, clear intent
4. Contact Close Exchange phone/handle with context Ghosting after giving number Seed a specific shared idea before asking
5. Text & Dates 1–3 Convert chat into real meetings Endless texting or 0 second dates Short callback text; two-stop date; lead pacing
6. Escalation & Sex Build physical chemistry respectfully "Friendly vibe" / friendzone Progressive touch ladder + read enthusiastic cues
7. Relationship Choose compatible partner & sustain bond Repeated anxious/avoidant drama Attachment vetting + turning toward daily bids

2. First-Impression Calibration: Warmth Before Competence

Dimension What It Signals High-Signal Behaviors Common Mistake
Warmth (Trust First) "I am safe, grounded, and friendly." Hands visible, relaxed shoulders, genuine Duchenne smile, unhurried pace Hands hidden in pockets, tense stare, rushing into credentials
Competence / Intent "I have direction and genuine romantic interest." Clear voice, calm eye contact, honest observation, unattached to outcome Faking bravado (micro-leaks), agreeing with everything, hiding intent

3. Real-Time Receptivity Signal Matrix

Signal Tier Verbal & Non-Verbal Cues Calibrated Action
Green (Turning Toward) Feet/torso angle toward you, asks follow-up questions, shares personal detail, removes headphones, smiles Stay present, deepen conversation, transition naturally to contact or next step
Ambiguous (Warm-Up) Polite but brief answer, slight surprise, neutral posture (common in quiet venues or high-context cultures) Offer low-pressure context hook + easy exit; reassess after 1–2 exchanges
Hard Stop (Closed / Indirect Decline) One-word replies, closed posture angled away, looking at phone/door, or soft evasion ("very busy this month") Respect immediately: smile, wish her well, and exit cleanly without pushing
