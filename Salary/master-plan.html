1. Your Multi-Job Compensation Roadmap (Job 1 → Upper-Band Principal)

With 1 YoE today at Johnson Health Tech earning $85k base + $5k bonus ($90k TC), you sit squarely inside Tier 1 (Traditional / Hardware-Fitness Enterprise). By 2027 you will hold ~2 YoE on the Junior (L3) / Early-Mid (L4) boundary. Your biggest single pay jump comes from crossing trimodal tiers into Tier 2 Mid-Tech or Tier 3 Big Tech while keeping a strict information firewall around $90k (patio11; Haseeb Rule 3)—and your long-term ceiling comes from combining smart external jumps with internal rating/refresher compounding.

Two Critical Rules for Job 1 → Job 2
1. The $90k Information Firewall: Never tell a Tier 2 or Tier 3 recruiter you earn $85k + $5k. A "generous" 20% bump on $90k lands at $108k—far below the $155k–$290k bands already budgeted.
2. Protect Your 2026–2027 Bridge: Time your Job 2 start date *after* your $5,000 annual bonus clears your bank account, build a quantified Android brag doc (Compose, BLE/hardware reliability, architecture), and prep DSA + Mobile System Design strictly outside work hours and devices.

3. End-to-End Negotiation Lifecycle (The 7 Phases)

Negotiation does not start when you receive a PDF offer—it starts months before you apply (Patrick McKenzie; Haseeb Qureshi). Print the quick cheat sheet in Pay Bands & Steps Reference.

Phase When It Happens Mid-Tech (Tier 2) Focus Big Tech (Tier 3) Focus Key Rule
Phase 1: Benchmark & Tax Before applying Model Base + Bonus + realistic 409A option EV, 90-day PTEW, and geo-tier band. Map L3/L4 level bands, 4-yr RSU schedules, 22% tax withholding, and Zone A–C multipliers. Know your COL- and tax-adjusted real value before talking.
Phase 2: Screen Firewall Recruiter call & forms Deflect "desired salary"; distinguish internal vs. 3rd-party agency recruiters. Extract the specific level & location range inside wide pay-transparency postings. Never disclose $90k current pay or your minimum walk-away number first.
Phase 3: Leveling Pre-loop & interviews Ensure you are scheduled for SWE II (not SWE I); show end-to-end Android ownership. Hit bar on DSA + Mobile System Design + BLE/Compose architecture to prevent L3 down-level. A one-level bump (L3 → L4) beats a 10% haggle inside a lower band.
Phase 4: Pipeline & BATNA Sourcing & Scheduling Time stealth interviews cleanly outside JHT hours; apply 2–3 wks after Big Tech. Start slower 45-day Big Tech loops first so offers land in one 5–10 day window. Never resign before your $5k bonus clears; real BATNA drives bidding.
Phase 5: Offer & Counter Verbal offer → 48–72h Get 409A/shares/vesting in writing; counter Base > Equity > Sign-On in a forwardable email. Anchor high using offer %ile + BATNA; swap capped base into Sign-On + extra 4-yr RSUs. State one exact number (never a range) backed by a concrete business case.
Phase 6: Objections & Fine Print Live calls → Signing Use Pause/Mirror/Defer on phone pushback; audit cliff clawbacks & PIIA Exhibit A. Verify vest-date cutoffs, bonus pro-ration, and reneging trade-offs cleanly. Never cave live on a call or move goalposts after agreeing on "final."
Phase 7: Compound to Top End Year 1 → L7+ Win 1.5x+ rating multipliers, pre-budget raises, and avoid retention counter-offers. Model Year-5 RSU cliffs, promo-then-jump to L5/L6, make-whole grants & severance. Stack internal progression with well-timed external resets all the way to L7+.
