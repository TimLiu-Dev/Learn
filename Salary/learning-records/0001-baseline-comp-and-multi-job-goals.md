# Baseline Compensation ($90k Tier 1, 1 YoE) & Multi-Job Target Arc

The learner currently has 1 year of experience (Nov 2026) at Johnson Health Tech making $85,000 base + $5,000 bonus ($90,000 TC), placing their baseline in Tier 1 (Traditional / Non-Tech Enterprise). By 2027 they will hold ~2 YoE—putting them directly on the Junior (L3) / Early-Mid (L4) boundary for Tier 2 Mid-Tech and Tier 3 Big Tech—so disclosing current $90k pay during Job 2 screens would severely suppress their anchor.

## Implications
- **Information Firewall (Lesson 04):** Never reveal $85k/$90k to Job 2 recruiters; anchor strictly to the target company's Tier 2/3 L3–L4 band ($150k–$240k+).
- **Tier-Jump Readiness (Lessons 05 & 09):** Moving from Tier 1 to Tier 2/3 requires highlighting production Android architecture/Compose ownership to avoid down-leveling, plus comparing internal-promo math against external tier-jump gains.
- **IP Hygiene (Capstone):** Because the learner is currently employed, the OfferLedger portfolio project should be built strictly on personal devices/time.
