Total Compensation, Bonus Cutoffs & Trimodal Pay Bands

Before you ever utter a counter-offer number, you must translate any recruiter's pitch into exact Year 1–4 cashflow and locate where it sits inside the company's true pay band.

Skill Unlocked: Offer Decomposition & Band Percentile Scoring
Career Arc: Job 1 ($90k) → Job 2 (2027) → Principal (L7+)
Payoff: Never confuse a one-time sign-on with recurring pay

1. Why "Total Compensation" Is Not One Number

When a recruiter says "We're offering $190,000 in Year-1 Total Compensation," two completely different packages can hide behind that single headline:

  • Package A (Steady Recurring): $150,000 base + $15,000 target bonus (10%) + $25,000/yr even-vesting RSUs = $190,000 every year for Years 1–4.
  • Package B (Sign-On Padded Cliff): $140,000 base + $0 bonus + $45,000 Year-1 sign-on + 5% of a $100,000 RSU grant ($5,000) in Year 1 = $190,000 in Year 1, dropping to $155,000 in Year 2 unless sign-on or refreshers bridge the gap.
  • Year-1 Bonus Pro-Ration & Cutoffs: Most companies pro-rate annual cash bonuses by months worked—and joining after an October 1 cutoff can yield $0 Year-1 bonus unless you negotiate a guaranteed Year-1 payout or sign-on bridge.
Haseeb's First Rule in Practice
Always get every component in writing before evaluating an offer (Haseeb Qureshi): Base Salary, Target Bonus % & Pro-Ration Cutoff, Total 4-Year Equity Grant, Annual Vesting Schedule (e.g., 25/25/25/25 vs. 5/15/40/40), and Year-1 / Year-2 Sign-On Cash.

2. The Trimodal Pay Band Map (Zone-A Hubs vs. Geo-Tiers)

As Gergely Orosz showed using Levels.fyi data, tech compensation is trimodal: three separate talent markets operate on different pay bands even for identical Android engineering titles. Your current role at Johnson Health Tech ($85k + $5k = $90k) sits in Tier 1; Job 2 targets Tier 2 or Tier 3.

3. Worked Offer Breakdown Example (Mid-Tech Offer)

Scenario: You receive a Tier 2 Mid-Tech Android offer: $138,000 Base + 10% Target Bonus + $60,000 4-Year RSUs (even 25%/yr) + $12,000 Year-1 Sign-On.

  • Annual Bonus: $138,000 × 10% = $13,800/yr
  • Annual RSU Vest: $60,000 × 25% = $15,000/yr
  • Year 1 TC: $138,000 + $13,800 + $15,000 + $12,000 = $178,800
  • Years 2–4 Recurring TC: $138,000 + $13,800 + $15,000 = $166,800/yr (a -$12,000 Year-2 drop)
  • Band Percentile inside a $130k–$175k Tier-2 Junior band (using $166,800 recurring): ($166,800 − $130,000) / $45,000 = 81.8% (Top-of-Band)
