Staff & Principal Negotiation (L6–L8+, Make-Whole, Tenders & Severance)

At Staff (L6, $580k–$780k+) and Principal (L7+, $900k–$1.4M+), base salaries flatten while 60%–75% of your package lives in equity. Switching jobs means walking away from hundreds of thousands in unvested RSUs—unless you negotiate a Make-Whole Grant, tender literacy, severance, and IP carve-outs.

Skill Unlocked: Unvested Make-Whole Bridges, Tender Math & Executive Contracts
Career Arc: Job 4 (Staff L6) → Job 5+ (Principal L7+)
Payoff: Never take a Year-1 pay cut when switching at Staff or Principal

1. The Four Pillars of L6–L8+ Negotiation

  • Make-Whole Grants (Cash + Equity Bridges): Suppose you hold $180,000 in unvested RSUs vesting over the next 12 months and $108,000 in Months 13–24. Show your new recruiter a vesting screenshot and negotiate a Year-1 Sign-On bridge ($180k cash) plus Year-2 Sign-On or front-loaded/extra RSUs (if the new grant vests 25%/yr, $108k × 2 = $216k covers Year 2; if back-weighted like 5/15/40/40, demand Year-1+Year-2 cash!).
  • Late-Stage Private & AI Lab Tender Offers: At frontier labs or decacorns (OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Stripe), equity may be PPUs or double-trigger RSUs with periodic company-run tender offers—verify tender cadence, blackout rules, and ordinary-income vs. capital-gains withholding before treating paper like liquid public RSUs.
  • Scope Charter, Year-1 Minimums & Severance: At Principal L7+, negotiate written team charter/headcount commitments, a 6-month refresher floor, and severance triggers (4–6 months base + COBRA + partial accelerated vest upon termination without cause or acquisition).
  • PIIA / Prior-Inventions IP Carve-Outs: From Job 2 through Principal, list independent personal projects on the employer's Prior Inventions Schedule (Exhibit A of your PIIA) before signing so your side work remains legally yours.
