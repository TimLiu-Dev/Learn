Refreshers, Year-5 Cliffs & Promo-Then-Jump Math

Once you land Job 2 in Mid-Tech or Big Tech, a silent trap appears in Year 4 and Year 5: your initial 4-year RSU grant runs out. Mastering stacked refreshers—and timing an internal promo before an external jump—unlocks Senior (L5) and Staff (L6) wealth.

Skill Unlocked: Stacked Refresher Forecasting & Promo-Then-Jump Timing
Career Arc: Job 2 → Job 3 (Senior L5) → Staff (L6)
Payoff: Spot the Year-5 equity drop 18 months early

1. How Stacked Equity Refreshers Work (and Why Year 5 Drops)

In Big Tech (Levels.fyi), engineers receive annual equity refreshers—new 4-year grants layering 25%/yr on top of existing grants. Because Year 4 stacks your big initial grant plus three refreshers, Year 4 TC peaks—then drops in Year 5 when the hiring grant expires:

Worked Example: $170k Base + 15% Bonus ($25.5k) = $195,500 Cash + Initial $160k RSU ($40k/yr for Y1–Y4) + Annual $60k Refresher ($15k/yr starting Y2):

  • Year 1 Equity: $40,000 (initial only) → Total = $235,500
  • Year 4 Equity: $40,000 + $15k + $15k + $15k = $85,000 (Peak!) → Total = $280,500
  • Year 5 Equity: $0 (initial expired!) + $15k + $15k + $15k + $15k = $60,000 → Total = $255,500 (-$25,000 cliff!)

2. Internal Promo Compression vs. "Promo-Then-Jump"

As r/ExperiencedDevs data shows, internal promotions inside one company typically yield a 10%–18% bump (leaving you compressed near the bottom of the next band). Moving externally resets your anchor to market 50th–85th percentile—AND the strongest move when close to Senior (L5) or Staff (L6) is often "Promo-Then-Jump": capture the internal L5 promotion first (proving L5 scope on your resume), then jump externally into a top-of-band Tier-3 L5 offer 6–12 months later!
