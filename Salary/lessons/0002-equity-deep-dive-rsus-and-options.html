Equity Deep Dive: RSUs, Double-Trigger Grants & Startup Options

As you move from Job 1 ($90k cash-only) into Tier 2 Mid-Tech and Tier 3 Big Tech—and later into Senior, Staff, and Principal—equity grows from 15% to 75% of your pay. Mispricing it is the fastest way to overvalue a weak offer.

Skill Unlocked: RSU Cliff Modeling & Startup Option Expected Value
Career Arc: Job 2 (2027 Mid/Big Tech) → Principal L7+
Payoff: Never treat illiquid startup options like public cash

1. Public RSUs vs. Double-Trigger RSUs vs. Startup Options

Not all "equity" works the same way. When evaluating Job 2+ offers (Levels.fyi; Carta Stock Options Guide), separate three distinct instruments:

  • Public RSUs (Big Tech & Public Mid-Tech): Restricted Stock Units convert directly into sellable shares on vest with no strike price. Share counts are usually fixed at hire using a 30-day trailing average stock price. Even if stock falls 30%, vested RSUs still hold real cash value.
  • Double-Trigger Private RSUs (Late-Stage Decacorns): Used by companies like Stripe or Databricks—units require both a time-vesting trigger AND a liquidity trigger (IPO or acquisition) before converting to sellable shares, protecting you from owing taxes on illiquid paper.
  • Startup Options (ISOs / NSOs): You do not own shares—you own the right to buy shares at a fixed strike price (set by the 409A Fair Market Value). Profit is max(0, Exit Price − Strike Price), shrunk further by future funding dilution and preferred VC liquidation preferences.
  • The 1-Year Cliff & Fixed Vest Dates: Almost every first-year equity grant has a 1-year cliff (leaving at month 11 yields $0). Also check whether subsequent vests hit quarterly on fixed company dates (e.g., Feb 15 / May 15 / Aug 15 / Nov 15)—missing a start-date cutoff can push your first vest out by 3 months!
4 Questions to Ask Any Startup Offering Stock Options
Before valuing a startup option pitch, ask the recruiter: (1) How many total diluted shares exist? (2) What is my exact strike price (409A)? (3) What was the latest preferred round valuation? (4) What is the post-termination exercise window (90 days vs. 7–10 years)? Treat illiquid options at a steep discount when comparing against public RSUs or base salary.

2. Worked Comparison Example

Scenario: Startup A offers 10,000 options at a $4.00 strike price. What is your gross option payout across three outcomes?

  • Down-round exit at $3.00/share: Underwater ($3 − $4 < 0) → $0.
  • Modest exit at $7.00/share: ($7 − $4) × 10,000 = $30,000 gross (before taxes & dilution).
  • Strong exit at $14.00/share: ($14 − $4) × 10,000 = $100,000 gross.
