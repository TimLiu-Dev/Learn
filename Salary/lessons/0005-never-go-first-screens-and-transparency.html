Never Go First: Screens, Agency Recruiters & Wide Transparency Bands

The #1 financial mistake when jumping from Johnson Health Tech ($85k + $5k = $90k) to Mid-Tech or Big Tech is answering "What are you making now?" or "What are your salary expectations?" before they want to hire you.

Skill Unlocked: Information Asymmetry, Wide-Band Decoding & Slip Recovery
Career Arc: Job 2 (2027 Phone Screens) → Principal L7+
Payoff: Prevent a $90k Tier-1 anchor from capping a $180k+ offer

1. The Three Core Rules of Recruiter Screening

  • Enforce the $90k Firewall (Haseeb Rule 3): In many US states (CA, NY, WA, CO, IL, MN, etc.), asking current salary is illegal. Even where legal, never share $90k. Your Tier-1 pay reflects your old market, not Tier 2/3 Android value.
  • Never State a Number First (patio11): Before interviews, you have zero leverage—the recruiter is screening out outliers. Your sole goal is advancing to technical rounds without setting a low ceiling.
  • Decode Wide Transparency Ranges: Many job posts list wide spans like $125,000 – $225,000 that secretly blend L3 + L4 + multiple Geo-Tiers. Ask: "Could you share which portion of that posted range corresponds to L4 / SWE II in my location, plus how target bonus and RSUs stack on top?"

2. Internal vs. Agency Recruiters, Slip-Ups & Background Verification

  • Internal vs. 3rd-Party Contingency Recruiters: Internal recruiters are salaried and rewarded for closing hires cleanly inside band. Contingency agency recruiters earn 15%–25% of your Year-1 base—so they actually want higher base pay, but often push harder for a number early to prevent submission rejections. Tell agency recruiters: "I'm targeting market top-of-band for L4/SWE II based on Levels.fyi—please anchor near the upper third of the client's budget."
  • If You Already Slipped Up & Gave a Low Number: Recover cleanly post-onsite: "Now that I've learned the full Android architecture scope in the technical rounds—and compared against other late-stage loops—I'm targeting market comp for this level rather than my earlier screen estimate."
  • Background Check & "The Work Number" Myth: Post-offer employment verification checks dates and job titles, not past W-2 pay—and you can freeze your Equifax The Work Number profile or redact dollar figures if ever asked for employment proof.
Exact Script for Online Forms & Pushed Calls
  • Online form requiring numbers: Enter 0 or 999999 if accepted, or enter the upper half of their posted transparency band (never $90k) and clarify on the call that total comp depends on level and equity.
  • If pushed a second time: "I'm confident that if we're a mutual fit on the Android team, we'll find a package inside your market band that works for both sides—what is the target base range for this specific level?"
