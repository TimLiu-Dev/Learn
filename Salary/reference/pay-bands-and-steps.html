Android Developer Compensation Rule
At Mid-Tech and Big Tech companies, Junior/Mid Android Engineers sit on the standard Software Engineer (SWE) pay band—there is no structural "mobile discount" when you pass coding + mobile system design loops (Levels.fyi). At Senior/Staff/Principal (L5–L7+), deep mobile specialization (Compose, KMP, BLE/hardware integration, app-wide reliability) creates scarcity leverage.

3. The 7-Phase End-to-End Salary Negotiation Steps

Phase Core Objective Primary Rule & Action Taught In
1. Market & Tax Calibration Know your net, tax-adjusted, geo-tiered band before applying. Model Base + Bonus + 4-yr RSU schedule + Sign-on + 22% withholding/AMT traps (Levels.fyi). L01 – L04
2. Screen Deflection Protect information asymmetry early. Never disclose $90k current pay or minimum floor; verify wide ranges & agency recruiter incentives (patio11). Lesson 05
3. Level Defense Maximize leveling signal during interviews. Lock L4 loop before interviews; frame Android architecture, BLE, Compose & reliability ownership. Lesson 06
4. Pipeline & BATNA Never negotiate with only one offer on the table. Stagger loops to overlap within 5–10 days; protect your JHT $5k bonus payout date (Haseeb Rule 6). Lesson 07
5. Counter & Lever Swap Anchor high with a forwardable business case. Send a Josh Doody email; prioritize Base > RSUs > Sign-On and swap capped base into cash + stock. Lesson 08
6. Objections & Closing Handle recruiter pushback without folding. Use Pause / Mirror / Defer on live calls, evaluate "best & final" against BATNA, audit clawbacks & start dates. Lesson 09
7. Inside & Multi-Job Compounding Capture top-end pay from L4 to L7+. Maximize rating multipliers, avoid retention counter-offer traps, forecast Year-5 cliffs, negotiate make-whole grants & severance. L10 – L12
