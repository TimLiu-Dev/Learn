Salary Negotiation Glossary

Canonical negotiation vocabulary used across all 12 lessons, Capstone 13, and the OfferLedger calculator.

Term Definition Introduced In
Total Compensation (TC) Actual annual economic value of an offer in a given year: Base + Target Bonus + Vested Equity + Sign-On Cash. Lesson 01
Trimodal Distribution Market structure where SWE pay clusters into Tier 1 (Traditional), Tier 2 (Mid-Tech), and Tier 3 (Big Tech/Frontier) bands (Pragmatic Engineer). Lesson 01
Band Percentile / Compa-Ratio Where an offer sits inside a level's min-to-max pay band (or relative to midpoint); near 90–100% leaves minimal raise headroom. Lesson 01
RSU & Double-Trigger RSU Stock units converting into shares on vest with no strike price. Pre-IPO decacorns often add a liquidity event trigger before shares settle. Lesson 02
ISO / NSO & 409A Strike Price Startup options giving the right to buy shares at a fixed 409A valuation strike price; value is max(0, ExitPrice - Strike). Lesson 02
Cliff (1-Year & Year-5) First-year date before which 0% equity vests, or the Year-5 TC drop when an initial 4-year RSU grant expires. L02 / L11
Clawback Clause (Pro-Rated vs. Cliff) Contract term requiring repayment of sign-on or relocation cash if you leave within 12–24 months; cross-year repayments often demand gross pre-tax dollars. Lesson 03
22% Supplemental Withholding IRS Pub 15 default federal tax withholding on RSU vests and bonuses (up to $1M), often below your 24%–37% marginal tax bracket. Lesson 04
90-Day PTEW & ISO AMT Post-Termination Exercise Window requiring cash purchase of vested startup options within 90 days of leaving, plus potential Alternative Minimum Tax on paper spread. Lesson 04
Geographic Pay Tier (Geo-Tier) Location-based cost-of-labor multiplier discounting Remote/MCOL offers by 10%–25% relative to SF/NYC/Seattle headquarters bands. Lesson 04
Information Firewall Strict rule against revealing prior compensation (e.g., $90k Tier 1) or minimum floor before receiving a written offer. Lesson 05
Down-Leveling Offering one engineering band lower than your experience/target (e.g., L3 instead of L4), cutting long-term TC ceilings. Lesson 06
BATNA Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement—your real outside offers or current $90k job that anchor your walk-away power. Lesson 07
Lever Swapping Converting a capped base salary ask into equivalent Year-1 sign-on cash and extra 4-year RSUs. Lesson 08
Pause / Mirror / Defer Verbal call tactic to acknowledge a recruiter objection warmly and move number commitments to calm written follow-up. Lesson 09
Rating Multiplier & Retention Counter Performance score multiplier (0.5x–2.0x) on annual bonus/refreshers, vs. a current-employer match offered only after you resign. Lesson 10
Refresher Annual recurring 4-year equity grant layered on top of existing unvested RSUs during performance cycles. Lesson 11
Make-Whole Grant Extra sign-on cash and front-loaded equity compensating you for unvested RSUs forfeited when switching companies. Lesson 12
PIIA Carve-Out Schedule listing prior personal/open-source projects (e.g., OfferLedger) to legally exclude them from employer IP assignment. Lesson 12
