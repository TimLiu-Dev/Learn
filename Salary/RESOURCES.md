# Salary Negotiation Resources

## Knowledge

- [Essay: "Salary Negotiation: Make More Money, Be More Valued" — Patrick McKenzie (patio11)](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2012/01/23/salary-negotiation/)
  Foundational essay on why companies expect software engineers to negotiate, never giving a number first, and negotiating across multiple dimensions. Use for: mindset, pre-offer rules, and recruiter incentives.
- [Essay: "Ten Rules for Negotiating a Job Offer" — Haseeb Qureshi](https://haseebq.com/my-ten-rules-for-negotiating-a-job-offer/)
  Tactical playbook covering information asymmetry, external decision-makers, BATNA/competing offers, and staying warm/positive. Use for: verbal jujitsu, exploding offers, and multi-offer leverage.
- [Book / Guide: _Fearless Salary Negotiation_ — Josh Doody](https://fearlesssalarynegotiation.com/)
  Step-by-step framework for calculating counter-offers and writing forwardable counter-offer emails to recruiters and hiring managers. Use for: written counter-offer templates and approval-chain framing.
- [Article: "The Trimodal Nature of Software Engineering Salaries" — Gergely Orosz (The Pragmatic Engineer)](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/software-engineering-salaries-in-the-netherlands-and-europe/)
  Explains why identical engineering titles pay 2x–5x differently across Tier 1 (local/traditional), Tier 2 (mid-tech/scale-ups), and Tier 3 (Big Tech/top-paying global firms). Use for: market tiering and band expectations.
- [Data Platform: Levels.fyi Compensation & Leveling Data](https://www.levels.fyi/)
  Crowdsourced, verified leveling equivalencies (e.g., Google L3–L7, Meta E3–E7, Amazon L4–L7, Microsoft 59–67), geographic pay-tier differentials, and base/equity/bonus/refresher breakdowns. Use for: up-to-date pay bands across Junior, Mid, Senior, Staff, and Principal engineers.
- [Tax Reference: IRS Publication 15 (Circular E) — Supplemental Wages](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15)
  Official federal payroll rule establishing the 22% optional flat withholding rate on supplemental wages (RSU vests, bonuses, sign-on up to $1M) and 37% mandatory rate above $1M. Use for: RSU tax-withholding planning and avoiding April tax surprises.
- [Equity Reference: Carta Equity & Stock Option Guides (409A, ISOs, AMT, 83(b), PTEW)](https://carta.com/learn/equity/stock-options/)
  Authoritative breakdown of ISO/NSO exercise mechanics, 90-day post-termination exercise windows (PTEW), Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) phantom-income triggers, and 83(b) deadlines. Use for: startup option diligence and avoiding golden handcuffs.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ExperiencedDevs](https://www.reddit.com/r/ExperiencedDevs/)
  High-signal community for software engineers (3+ YoE) discussing leveling, down-leveling, promo cycles, retention counter-offers, and compensation strategy. Use for: Senior/Staff/Principal calibration and real-world offer reviews.
- [r/cscareerquestions](https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions/)
  Large community focused on entry-level/new-grad and early-career software engineering recruiting, timelines, and offer comparisons. Use for: 2027 Junior SWE/Android market pulse.
- [r/androiddev](https://www.reddit.com/r/androiddev/)
  Active Android developer community covering Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, interview loops, and mobile career progression. Use for: Android-specific market signals and portfolio feedback.
- [TeamBlind](https://www.teamblind.com/)
  Verified-employer anonymous forum heavily used by Mid-Tech and Big Tech engineers for live TC data points, recruiter tactics, refresher ratings, and offer evaluations. Use for: verifying current Tier 2 / Tier 3 band ceilings (filter out cynicism).
