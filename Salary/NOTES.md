# Teaching Notes & User Preferences

## Learner Profile & Target Arc
- **Current Baseline (Job 1, Nov 2026):** 1 YoE at Johnson Health Tech — $85,000 base + $5,000 bonus = **$90,000 TC** (Tier 1 Traditional/Hardware-Fitness Enterprise).
- **Job 2 Target (2027, ~2 YoE):** Positioned on the L3/L4 boundary — jump to **Tier 2 Mid-Tech** ($155k–$215k+ TC) or **Tier 3 Big Tech L3/L4** ($185k–$290k+ TC). Critical rules: never disclose current $90k comp, protect JHT's $5k annual bonus timing, account for geo-tiering, and defend against L3 down-leveling when eligible for SWE II / L4.
- **Job 3 Target (2029–2031):** **Senior Android (L5)** — Tier 2 ($250k–$330k+) or Tier 3 ($380k–$500k+), leveraging rating multipliers and promo-then-jump sequencing.
- **Job 4+ Target (Long-Term):** **Staff (L6)** upper band ($580k–$780k+) → **Principal (L7+)** upper band ($900k–$1.4M+).

## Explicit Format & Pedagogical Preferences
1. **Pure Salary Negotiation (NO coding exercises):** Focus 100% on everything related to salary negotiation and career-long compensation progression (offer math, pay bands, geo-tiering, equity & equity taxes, net COL/benefits, scripts, deflection, leveling, BATNA, counter-offers, objections, inside progression/rating multipliers/retention offers, refreshers, make-whole grants, severance).
2. **Master Plan Legend first:** Comprehensive end-to-end `master-plan.html` acting as a legend for all 12 lessons + Capstone 13, 7 negotiation phases, geo-tiered pay bands (Mid-Tech & Big Tech), and multi-job targets.
3. **Interactive Practice in HTML:** Interactive **Negotiation & Compensation Practice Labs** (real offer calculations, 3–4 option scenario decisions, script builders) that run/check in the browser with **Hints** and **Final Answer** reveal. Avoid 2-option 50/50 dropdowns.
4. **Multiple-Choice Quizzes:** Every quiz uses balanced-length multiple-choice questions and shows **why the correct answer is right AND why each wrong answer is wrong**.
5. **Teacher Prompt:** Every lesson ends with a reminder to ask follow-up questions to the agent.
6. **End-to-End Capstone:** Interactive Offer Evaluator, Counter-Offer Generator & Personal Negotiation Playbook (`offerledger/index.html` + Lesson 13).
