# Mission: End-to-End Tech Salary Negotiation (Job 1 $90k -> Upper-Band Principal)

## Why
Break out of Tier 1 compensation (currently 1 YoE at Johnson Health Tech making $85k base + $5k bonus = $90k TC) into a top-of-band Junior/Early-Mid (L3–L4, ~2 YoE) Android Developer offer in 2027 (Job 2), then master both **internal progression** (rating multipliers, off-cycle raises, promo-then-jump timing) and **external tier/level jumps** across Job 3 (Senior), Job 4 (Staff), and Job 5+ (upper-band Principal) in Mid-Tech and Big Tech.

## Success looks like
- **Job 1 Bridge (Now – 2027):** Protect your $5k bonus payout date, document Android architecture/Compose/BLE wins in a brag doc, and enforce a strict $90k Information Firewall during Job 2 screens.
- **Job 2 (2027 Jump, ~2 YoE):** Defend against down-leveling below your 2 YoE mark, account for geo-tiering and equity/tax fine print, and land top-of-band Tier 2 Mid-Tech ($155k–$215k+ TC) or Tier 3 Big Tech L3/L4 ($185k–$290k+ TC).
- **Job 3 (Mid -> Senior, ~2–4 yrs post-Job 2):** Capture L4/L5 Senior band ($250k–$330k+ Tier 2 or $380k–$500k+ Tier 3) via rating multipliers, promo-then-jump sequencing, competing offers, or high-impact tier jump.
- **Job 4+ (Senior -> Staff -> Principal):** Reach upper-band Staff L6 ($580k–$780k+) and Principal L7+ ($900k–$1.4M+) using equity/refresher optimization, make-whole grants, tender-offer literacy, severance, and PIIA carve-outs.
- **End-to-End Playbook & Command Center:** Complete the full 12-lesson + Capstone curriculum and live Offer Evaluator / Counter-Offer Simulator (`offerledger/index.html`) ready for every future raise, promo, and job switch.

## Constraints
- Pure salary negotiation curriculum (no programming/coding exercises required)—interactive HTML lessons use real-world offer math, scenario simulators, scripts, progressive hints, final answers, and balanced multiple-choice quizzes explaining why right and wrong answers work.
- Self-contained HTML files in `./lessons/` reusing `./assets/` components.
- Currently employed full-time at Johnson Health Tech ($85k + $5k bonus); keep job search & prep cleanly outside work hours and devices.

## Out of scope
- Software engineering / coding assignments (not a coding project).
- Personal tax filing or legal counsel (modeling marginal taxes, 22% RSU withholding, ISO AMT, ESPP/401k, and spotting clawback/PIIA clauses for CPA/attorney review is in scope).
