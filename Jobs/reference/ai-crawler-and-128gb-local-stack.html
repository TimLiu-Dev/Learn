3. Auto-Apply & Local LLM Survival Rules

  • Always pass response_format={"type": "json_schema", ...} (or GBNF grammars) on local 30B–120B servers so Pydantic parsing never breaks.
  • Never fill hidden/zero-opacity DOM inputs (ATS honeypots), and reuse Playwright storageState cookies rather than re-logging in per run.
  • Enforce strict subset validation: every skill claimed in a tailored resume must exist in your verified profile.yaml.
  • Cap daily velocity (5–15 high-fit apps/day) and pause cleanly for human handoff on CAPTCHAs, Turnstile, or 2FA gates.
