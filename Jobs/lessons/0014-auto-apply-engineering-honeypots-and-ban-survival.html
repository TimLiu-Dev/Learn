Auto-Apply Engineering, Honeypots & Ban Survival

Why naive mass-apply bots get LinkedIn accounts suspended and IP-blacklisted across Workday/Greenhouse — and how to engineer a high-speed Playwright/browser-use runner that actually lands interviews.

Primary Source & Citations

Grounded in browser-use / Playwright form automation patterns and ATS bot-detection realities.

1. Four Failure Modes That Kill Naive Auto-Appliers

  1. Hidden ATS Honeypots: Portals inject invisible fields (display:none, opacity:0, or off-screen coordinates). Blind scripts that iterate over every <input> fill the trap and get auto-blacklisted.
  2. Velocity & Session Bans: Rapid-fire Easy Apply clicks, repeated fresh logins (instead of reusing Playwright storageState cookies), or blasting 200 untargeted portals daily trigger behavioral analytics and account locks. Cap automated submissions to 5–15 high-fit roles/day.
  3. Hallucinated Skills: Letting an LLM rewrite your resume to claim 5 years of Kubernetes or Rust gets caught in live coding rounds and triggers recruiter blacklisting.
  4. Verification Gates: Attempting to defeat CAPTCHAs or access controls fails reliably and breaks platform boundaries; a production runner pauses cleanly and notifies you to complete any CAPTCHA/2FA gate manually.

2. Defensive Playwright + Truth-Assertion Pattern

# 1) Never touch invisible honeypot fields
visible = await locator.evaluate("el => el.offsetParent !== null && getComputedStyle(el).opacity !== '0'")

# 2) Enforce zero-hallucination guard on tailored resumes
assert set(tailored.skills).issubset(set(canonical_profile.verified_skills))

# 3) Pause cleanly for human handoff on CAPTCHA / 2FA / final submit review
if await detect_verification_gate(page):
    notify_user_for_handoff(job.url)
