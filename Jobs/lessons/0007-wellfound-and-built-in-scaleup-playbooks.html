Wellfound & Built In Scale-Up Playbooks

Your 2027 Mid-Tech bridge lives on Wellfound (Seed–Series C) and Built In (regional scale-ups). Learn how these ecosystems differ from Big Tech and how to pitch engineering leads directly.

1. Wellfound, Built In, YC & Levels.fyi: Distinct Advantages

  • Wellfound & YC Work at a Startup: Ideal for Pre-Seed through Series C startups. Filter directly by funding stage, equity, compensation band, remote/hybrid, and tech stack. Applications often land straight in a founder's, CTO's, or Lead Engineer's inbox.
  • Built In (Chicago, NYC, SF, Austin, etc.) & Levels.fyi Jobs: Ideal for your Mid-Tech 2 YoE target (Series B–D and regional scale-ups). Built In exposes engineering team size and core stack (great for spotting Kotlin + Spring Boot shops); Levels.fyi Jobs layers verified L4/L5 total-comp bands onto active listings.

2. Startup Runway Check & Pitching Ownership

Before joining a Seed/Series A startup at 2 YoE, vet burn vs. runway (18+ months post-raise, real revenue/customers, and whether senior engineers exist to mentor you toward L4/L5 rigor). Then pitch ownership concisely:
