1. Anatomy of Total Compensation (TC)

Annual_TC = Base_Salary + Target_Bonus + (Total_RSU_Grant * Year_Vest_Pct) + (Sign_On / Years)
# Watch Amazon-style back-loaded schedules (5% / 15% / 40% / 40% over 4 yrs + Year 1-2 cash sign-on)
# vs. standard 25% / 25% / 25% / 25% RSU vesting.

Key Negotiation Insight: Base salary bands are tight and require VP/comp-committee exceptions to break. RSU grants and one-time sign-on bonuses are the flexible levers recruiters use to close preferred engineers.

2. Startup Equity vs. Public RSUs (Wellfound Series A–C)

Since you also target Wellfound scale-ups in Lesson 0007, never price private stock options at 100% face value like public RSUs: check the 409A strike price, ISO vs. NSO tax treatment, 1-year cliff, and post-termination exercise window (90 days vs. 7–10 years). Discount illiquid startup paper heavily when comparing against liquid Big Tech cash + RSUs.

4. The Commitment Counter-Offer Frame

Always anchor on a specific TC / equity + sign-on target backed by competing offers or unvested equity, and pair your ask with explicit willingness to sign: "If we can move the RSU grant to $X and sign-on to $Y, I am ready to sign today." Never fabricate competing offers — companies rescind offers immediately if caught.
