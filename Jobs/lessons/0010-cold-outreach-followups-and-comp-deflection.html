Cold Outreach, Follow-Ups & Comp Deflection

How to write 4-sentence technical outreach that internal recruiters actually answer, run polite follow-ups without burning bridges, and handle early compensation questions without anchoring yourself low.

Primary Source & Citations

Grounded in Tech Interview Handbook negotiation/recruiter-screen playbooks and Levels.fyi band awareness.

1. Three Tailored 4-Sentence Hooks (By Recipient)

Recruiters triage hundreds of messages a day, while Engineering Managers care about system ownership and reliability. Match the hook to the person:

  • Internal Sourcer / Recruiter: (1) Current JHT title/YoE → (2) Exact Role + Req ID → (3) 1 XYZ metric + stack keywords → (4) 10-min screen CTA.
  • Engineering Manager: (1) Shared domain/stack pain point → (2) Production reliability or latency win at JHT → (3) 60s link to SignalApply architecture/tests → (4) Low-friction ask.
  • Engineering Peer (Referral Path): (1) Shared alumni/community context → (2) 1 specific technical question about their Mobile/Spring Boot team → (3) Zero referral ask in message #1.
