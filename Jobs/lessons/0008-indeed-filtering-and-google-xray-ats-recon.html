Indeed Filtering & Google X-Ray ATS Recon

Indeed sweeps traditional and local mid-market companies that skip Wellfound, but it is flooded with stale ghost listings. Learn strict freshness filtering plus Google X-Ray queries that hit fresh ATS boards directly.

Primary Source & Citations

1. Treating Indeed as a Secondary Sweep & Pruning Ghost Jobs

Non-tech enterprises, healthcare/fitness tech firms, and regional companies routinely post on Indeed without touching niche tech boards. To avoid stale "ghost jobs":

  • Always lock Date Posted to Last 24 hours or Last 7 days (fromage=7).
  • Spot ghost-job signals: identical requisition IDs reposted every 30 days for months, generic "evergreen talent pipeline" wording, or listings absent from the company's own ATS career page.
  • Click through to the company's direct ATS whenever possible rather than relying on third-party Easy Apply mirrors.

2. Google X-Ray Search Across Direct ATS Domains (Including Workday)

You can find unadvertised or brand-new 2 YoE Android and Spring Boot openings hours before aggregators scrape them by querying Google directly against ATS hostnames: 

site:boards.greenhouse.io ("Android" OR "Spring Boot") ("2 years" OR "Mid") -Senior -Staff
site:jobs.lever.co ("Android Engineer" OR "Backend Engineer") (Kotlin OR Java)
site:jobs.ashbyhq.com ("Software Engineer" AND ("Kotlin" OR "Spring Boot"))
site:myworkdayjobs.com ("Android" OR "Spring Boot") ("Software Engineer") -Senior -Principal
