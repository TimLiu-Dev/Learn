1. Mobile, BFF & Backend System Design

  • Mobile Client Design (e.g., Offline-First Fitness / Feed App): Local Room single-source-of-truth, cursor pagination, image caching, WorkManager background sync with exponential backoff, battery/network constraints, and multi-module Gradle boundaries.
  • Backend & BFF Contract Design (Your Dual-Stack Edge): API contracts (cursor pagination, protobuf/REST versioning, mobile payload shaping), Redis caching, DB indexing/sharding, Transactional Outbox (solving DB + Kafka dual-write bugs), idempotency keys, and observability.

2. Senior Behavioral Calibration & Failure Modes

At L4 (Mid), stories show you executed features independently. At Senior L5/E5/L6, your STAR stories must prove ambiguity resolution, cross-team trade-offs, production outage ownership, and mentoring. Common failure modes at Google/Amazon: picking trivial bug fixes ("L3 scope"), describing "we" instead of your exact "I" decisions, or skipping measurable post-launch outcomes.
