Internal vs. Agency Recruiters: Incentives & Discovery

Recruiters are not all the same. Learn how internal sourcers, contingent agencies, and retained firms are compensated — and how to locate the exact recruiter owning Mobile or Backend requisitions at your target company.

Primary Source & Citations

Grounded in The Pragmatic Engineer hiring pipeline analyses and r/ExperiencedDevs recruiter consensus.

1. The 3 Recruiter Types & Their Incentives

  • Internal Corporate Sourcers / Recruiters (Mid-Tech & FAANG): Salaried employees evaluated on hiring-manager conversion rate and time-to-fill. They guard team quality and specialize by org (e.g., Mobile Engineering Recruiter, Backend/Infra Sourcer).
  • Contingent Agency Recruiters: Paid a percentage (often 15–25% of first-year base) only if their candidate gets hired. High urgency, fast feedback — but watch out for double-submission conflicts (never let two agencies submit you to the same company).
  • Retained Executive Search: Paid upfront to fill Staff/Principal/VP roles (relevant later above L5).

2. Finding Team Recruiters & Handling Contract Pitches

Use LinkedIn or Google X-Ray to pinpoint domain-aligned sourcers, then verify work-email formats (first.last@company.com) before outreach. When third-party agencies pitch W-2 hourly contract or C2C roles instead of FTE: ask upfront about duration, conversion-to-FTE rate, and benefits—at 2 YoE targeting Mid-Tech/FAANG growth, direct FTE usually beats short-term staffing contracts unless the company is a Tier-1 brand bridge.
