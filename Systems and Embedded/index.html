The Self-Taught Android Bridge: What We Added, Removed & Why

As a self-taught junior Android engineer, you know Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines, and Gradle. But low-level systems requires a different mental model. Here is how this curriculum eliminates the hidden traps and equips you for senior systems roles:

✔ WHAT WE ADDED

Track 00 (Silicon & C Bridge Foundations): Two's complement, binary arithmetic, endianness, the C compilation pipeline, plus a dedicated 4-lesson C11 bridge (<stdint.h>, qualifiers, pointers & bounds-safe buffers, ABI structs, function-pointer dispatch, headers/linkage, macros, Makefile, and ASan/UBSan).

Track 03 (Modern C++20 Bridge & Senior Debugging): Kotlin-to-C++ value semantics, named casts, Rule of Five, std::unique_ptr/move, exception-free std::optional/expected, C++20 concepts, extern "C" ABI, CMake + GoogleTest host testing, GDB, OpenOCD, objdump, and QEMU virtual labs.

✖ WHAT WE REMOVED

Legacy 8-bit Microcontrollers: No 8051, PIC, or Arduino AVR. Automotive and high-end systems use 32-bit and 64-bit ARM / RISC-V.

Pre-C++11 Obsolete Idioms: No raw pointers or complex class inheritance trees. We jump directly from C to modern C++20 with constexpr and std::span.

Deprecated Android HIDL: Android officially deprecated HIDL in Android 13; we focus strictly on modern AIDL HALs in C++ and Rust.

🚀 THE 2027 SENIOR BAR

What separates a Junior from a Senior:
• Junior: Writes code that works only on the happy path.
• Senior: Architects for hardware errata, thermal throttling, memory leaks without GC, watchdog recovery, race conditions under high interrupt load, and ASIL functional safety.

By combining your Android framework fluency with bare-metal, Linux kernel, and Rust skills, you bridge the gap between silicon and user screens.
