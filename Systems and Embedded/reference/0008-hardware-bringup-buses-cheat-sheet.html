Hardware Lab Bring-Up, Chip IDs, Traps & Bus Rules

Print-ready bench card for your 33-item (37-unit) 2027 hardware lab (nRF54L15-DK, STM32F411, Blue Pill, ESP32-S3/C3, Pi 4, TB6612FNG + 12GA-N20 motor, and soldering/test gear).

1. Mandatory Voltage & Pin TrapsDay 1
Board / ModuleCritical Check
nRF54L15-DKMay default to 1.8V I/O! Set to 3.3V in Board Configurator + DMM verify.
CP2102 UARTSet jumper/level to 3.3V before wiring to Raspberry Pi 4 UART!
ESP32-S3 N16R8Do NOT wire GPIO35–37 (used internally by Octal PSRAM).
ESP32-S3 eFusesUse CONFIG_EFUSE_VIRTUAL=y in labs; real eFuse burn is irreversible.

12GA-N20 + TB6612FNGEncoder VCC = 3.3V (MCU-safe); VM = 2nd MB-102 5V supply (common GND); pull STBY HIGH.
2. Silicon Identity & Bus Quirks0008a/b
PartVerify Register / Hardware Rule
BME280Reg 0xD0 == 0x60 (if 0x58, relabeled BMP280 without humidity!).
LSM6DSV16XWHO_AM_I (0x0F) == 0x70; INT1 triggers EXTI read.
0.96" SSD1306Ships in 4-wire SPI (1KB page buffer); I2C needs moving back resistors.
1.3" ST7789No CS pin, usually SPI Mode 3 → dedicate its own SPI bus!
MicroSDIf 74LVC125 + AMS1117 present: power 5V, keep off shared MISO bus.
3. CAN & RS485 Physical Bus Rules0016a
BusWiring & Scope Verification
CAN (SN65HVD230)2 nodes w/ 120Ω onboard = 60Ω unpowered; remove 3rd node's 120Ω!
RS485 (SP3485)Wait for UART TC (not TXE) before dropping DE/RE LOW.
DSO2512G ScopeUse 10x probes for SPI/xtal; CH1 - CH2 math for CAN/RS485 diff.
24MHz AnalyzerLower SPI clock (≤ 4 MHz) when decoding SD/TFT traffic in PulseView.
