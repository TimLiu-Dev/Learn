Mid-Tech Senior-Level: Technical Ownership & Tech Debt Paydown

Learn how to answer: "Tell me about a time you identified critical technical debt and convinced leadership to fund its refactoring." Master calculating engineering ROI in developer hours and pipeline costs.

⚖️ 4-Way Leveling Matrix: Tech Debt Ownership
"Our monolithic :app module was messy with a lot of deprecated AsyncTask and callback hell. During sprint hackathon, I spent three days converting 5 legacy repositories to Kotlin Coroutines and Room database. I created a PR with 2,000 lines of diff. The code was much cleaner and fewer crashes occurred."

✅ Strengths

  • Strong personal coding output and modern Kotlin adoption.

❌ Red Flags for Senior

  • 2,000-line PR is dangerous and unreviewable.
  • No buy-in from product or team; executed as rogue refactoring.
Verdict: Mid-Tech Mid-Level (L4)
"Our clean build times had degraded to 14.5 minutes, and incremental builds took 3.5 minutes. With 12 Android developers committing multiple times daily, our engineering team was losing ~18 developer-hours every single day to Gradle wait times. I prepared an executive business case for our Engineering Director, proving this build latency cost the company $140,000 annually in lost productivity. I secured agreement to allocate 20% of two consecutive sprints to modularization. I led the extraction of core network, database, and feature modules (`:core:network`, `:feature:auth`), configured Gradle configuration caching, and enabled remote build cache. Clean builds dropped from 14.5 min to 3.8 min (-74%), and incremental builds dropped to 18 seconds."

✅ High-Impact Senior Signals

  • Translates compilation time into real dollar business impact ($140K/yr).
  • Negotiates 20% sprint capacity formally rather than covert refactoring.
  • Sensational, verifiable metrics (74% build acceleration).
Verdict: Definitive Strong Hire for Mid-Tech Senior (IC5 / Lead)
"Our user profile screen had accumulated 4 years of legacy Java and RxJava spaghetti, causing frequent NullPointerExceptions on low-end devices. I isolated the domain logic, wrote comprehensive JUnit tests with 92% branch coverage, and incrementally migrated the screen to Kotlin Coroutines StateFlow behind a feature flag. Crash rate on that screen dropped to 0.01%."

✅ Strengths

  • Incremental, safe migration using feature flags and test coverage.
Verdict: Confident Pass for Big Tech Mid (L4/E4)
"Across our 90-person mobile org, binary size creep (+12MB in 2 quarters) and cold startup regressions were causing Google Play store ranking penalties. As Platform Tech Lead, I designed the 'Mobile Health Scorecard', tying build health, DEX method counts, and Macrobenchmark startup metrics to quarterly team OKRs. I spearheaded the adoption of R8 full-mode shrinking and Baseline Profiles across 18 feature teams, reducing APK download size by 28% and decreasing cold start time by 320ms across 80M devices."

✅ Big Tech L5/E5 Multi-Team Leadership

  • Creates institutional accountability ('Mobile Health Scorecard').
  • Influences 18 feature teams and impacts 80M users globally.
Verdict: Top-Tier Big Tech Senior (L5/E5)

💻 Interactive Code Practice: Configure Gradle Build Cache & Modularization

The technical solution: Optimize gradle.properties and module dependencies to prevent circular coupling and maximize parallel execution.

Gradle & Build Architecture
The Problem: The existing build has disabled caching, single-threaded execution, and bloated memory limits.

Your Task: Configure gradle.properties with:
  • org.gradle.caching=true
  • org.gradle.parallel=true
  • org.gradle.configuration-cache=true
  • Appropriate JVM heap args (e.g. -Xmx4096m).
gradle.properties Gradle Build Config
💡 Hint: Enable org.gradle.caching=true, org.gradle.parallel=true, and org.gradle.configuration-cache=true. Increase JVM heap to -Xmx4096m -XX:+UseParallelGC.
