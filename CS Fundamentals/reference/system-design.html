Consistency & CAP

Strongacts like one copy; reads see every finished write
Read-your-writesyou see your own writes; others may lag
Eventualcopies converge once writes stop
CAP ★only during a partition: refuse (C) or answer stale (A)
PACELCelse: latency vs consistency (sync vs async replicas)
Last write wins ★simple, converges, silently drops concurrent writes
Keep bothdetect siblings (same parent), merge (cart: union)
source: Brewer 2012 · Kleppmann 2015 · DDIA 2nd ed.
