A week at the market stall

Answer each question with one chain of collection operations: no loops.

  • unitsPerItem: total units per item, in first-seen order.
  • topItem: the item with the most units in total, or null when there are no sales.
  • splitBySize: the sales with at least min units, and the rest, in one call.
  • changes: the change from each day's total to the next: [5, 8, 6] gives [3, -2].
