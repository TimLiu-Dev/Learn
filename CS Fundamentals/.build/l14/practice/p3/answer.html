Why it works. Each question names its operation: "per item" is a grouping, "the most" is a max, "and the rest" is a partition, "each and the next" is a zip. topItem reuses unitsPerItem, so the totals are worked out in one place. Kotlin's maxByOrNull gives null where Java's max gives an empty Optional.

Common mistake. sales.maxByOrNull { it.qty }?.item. It finds the biggest single sale (jam, 6 units) instead of the biggest total (tea, 9 units). Read the question again: "in total" means group first.
