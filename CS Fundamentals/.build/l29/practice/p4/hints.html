kotlinx.coroutines.future.await is an extension on CompletableFuture (and any CompletionStage): future.await() suspends until the value is there. Call client.price for every item first, keep the futures in a list, and only then await them.

---

For the timeout, wrap total(...) in withTimeoutOrNull(timeoutMs) { … }. You don't have to cancel anything yourself: when the timeout cancels the coroutine, await() cancels the future it was waiting on.

---

Never call join() or get() on the future inside a coroutine: they block the thread, so the timeout can't interrupt the wait. In Java: CompletableFuture.allOf(...).get(timeoutMs, MILLISECONDS), and on TimeoutException, call cancel(true) on every future and return null.
