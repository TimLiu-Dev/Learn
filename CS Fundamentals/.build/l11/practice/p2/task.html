Test cases that catch bugs

This time you write only the test data. Fill gradeCases with rows of score to expectedGrade, like @CsvSource rows, for this spec: 90–100 is "A", 80–89 "B", 70–79 "C", 0–69 "F", and any other score is "invalid".

  • The checks run your rows against six buggy copies of grade, each off by one somewhere. A copy counts as caught when at least one of your (correct) rows gives a different answer.
  • Use at most 10 rows. Choosing them well is the skill.

The hidden checks import JUnit 4's org.junit.Test. In your Gradle projects it's org.junit.jupiter.api.Test.
