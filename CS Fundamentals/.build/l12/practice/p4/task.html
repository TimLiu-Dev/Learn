Fast-forward or merge commit?

The repository model is back, now with parents as a list, because a merge commit has two. commit, createBranch and switch are done. Write the two missing pieces:

  • isAncestor(a, b): can you reach commit a from b by following parent links? A commit counts as its own ancestor.
  • merge(other) merges branch other into the current branch and returns what happened: "up to date" (their commit is already in your history), "fast-forward" (move your pointer, make no commit), or "merge commit" (a new commit with parents your tip, their tip, in that order).

This is the decision git merge makes before it touches any file. Practise the real thing in your terminal with Part 4's exercise.
