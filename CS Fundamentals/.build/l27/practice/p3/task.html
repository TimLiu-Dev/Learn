Build your own ArrayBlockingQueue

L26 promised you Conditions: a wait queue tied to a lock. c.await() releases the lock and sleeps until another thread calls c.signalAll(), then takes the lock back before returning.

  • put(item) waits on notFull while the queue holds capacity items, then adds at the back.
  • take() waits on notEmpty while it's empty, then removes from the front.
  • Each one wakes the threads waiting on the other condition.

The checks count the threads waiting on each condition, so a thread that spins or returns early fails.
