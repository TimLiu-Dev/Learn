login    -> A: session 09ff6ad2
GET /me  -> B: 401 who are you? (B has no such session)
GET /me  -> A: 200 hello ada (from A)
-- stateless: a signed token, nothing remembered
login    -> A: token ada.cUwOwDHjKosyBHs2
GET /me  -> B: 200 hello ada (from B)
GET /me  -> A: 200 hello ada (from A)
forged   -> B: 401 bad signature
