Why it works. mod always lands in 0 … 1439, whatever the sign of its input. Reducing delta first is MCS's rule from Part 3, "take remainders at every step", and it gives the same answer as reducing once at the end, while keeping every intermediate value small enough that nothing overflows. halvings is the counting loop from Logs: each pass halves x, so the count is ⌊log₂ n⌋, and require rejects the n where "halve until 1" never ends.

Common mistakes. (start + delta) % 1440 returns −20 instead of 1420 when you go back past midnight. (start + delta).mod(1440) fixes the sign but still overflows on Int.MAX_VALUE, which is L2's overflow inside Part 3's maths.
