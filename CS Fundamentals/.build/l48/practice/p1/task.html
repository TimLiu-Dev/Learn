Prove it by checking every row

A boolean formula with two inputs has only four rows in its truth table, so checking all four is a real proof: a proof by exhaustion. Write three small tools for that:

  • implies(p, q), the implication p → q.
  • equivalent(f, g): do two formulas agree on all four input pairs?
  • alwaysTrue(f): is the formula a tautology, true on every row?

The checks use them to confirm the contrapositive, refute the converse and prove De Morgan's law.
