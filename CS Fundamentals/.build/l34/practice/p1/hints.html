Start from the reviews table in Part 1's cascade demo. It already has REFERENCES books(id) ON DELETE CASCADE and CHECK (stars BETWEEN 1 AND 5). Look at what's left in your result: which bad row got in anyway?

---

The review with stars NULL survives the CHECK. SQLite counts a CHECK as broken only when its test is false, and NULL BETWEEN 1 AND 5 is NULL (unknown), not false. A required column needs NOT NULL as well as its CHECK.

---

For body, that same rule is exactly what you want: CHECK (body <> '') rejects '' and lets NULL through. Don't add NOT NULL there, or the review with no body is lost. Two reviews should be left: book 1's five stars and book 2's four stars with no body.
