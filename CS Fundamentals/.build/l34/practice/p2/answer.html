Why it works. The parenthesised OR keeps the price test on every row, and IS NULL is the only test that's true for an unknown year. The sorted list is Beowulf, The Epic of Gilgamesh, Sir Gawain | Don Quixote, Frankenstein, Dracula | A Wizard of Earthsea, so page 2 is the middle three. The title tiebreak matters for the two undated books: without it, their order is undefined.

Common mistake. year = NULL. It's unknown for every row, so all the undated books vanish, page 1 starts at Sir Gawain, and page 2 shifts to Dracula and A Wizard of Earthsea. Leaving out the parentheses is the other classic: an undated book over $13 gets in.
