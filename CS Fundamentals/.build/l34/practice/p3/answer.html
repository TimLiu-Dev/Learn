Why it works. The LEFT JOIN keeps every customer. Because the status test is in ON, it only decides which orders match, so Chen (one cancelled order), Eli and Fay each keep one row with NULLs. count(o.id) counts non-NULL ids, giving them 0, and HAVING then drops Ana and Dara, who have 2 each.

Common mistake. Fixing only the join type. With the status test left in WHERE, the LEFT JOIN plus HAVING still returns just Ben, because WHERE turns the LEFT JOIN back into an inner one. And with count(*), the three customers with no paid orders show a count of 1.
