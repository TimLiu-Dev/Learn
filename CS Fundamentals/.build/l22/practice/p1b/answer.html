Why it works. It follows HotSpot's Arguments::set_heap_size: a tiny limit gets half of it, so the JVM can start at all; a normal limit gets MaxRAMPercentage of it, but never less than the old 125 MB default; and -Xmx skips the whole calculation. In a container the "limit" is the cgroup's memory limit, so the same three rules decide your heap there.

Common mistake. Believing "the heap is 25% of the limit" with no exceptions. That gives 32 MB for a 128 MB limit and 104 MB for 400 MB, where the real JVM picked 64 and 128. The "tiny limits" and "in between" checks catch it.
