Every way of making a Booking (the constructor, copy, your two factories) ends up in the primary constructor, so the rules belong in init. Use require(condition) { "message" }: it throws IllegalArgumentException (L10).

---

allDay is one line with named arguments: Booking(room, start = 8, end = 18). For parse, split(" ") gives the room, the hours and maybe "x4". Split the hours on "-", and use getOrNull(2) for the optional part.

---

parse needs no checks of its own: it calls the constructor, so init rejects "Ada 11-9". And "nine".toInt() throws NumberFormatException, which is a subclass of IllegalArgumentException. In Java, the same rules go in the record's compact constructor, Booking { … }, and the builder's build() just calls it.
