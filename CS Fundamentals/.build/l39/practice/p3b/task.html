A review workflow that refuses illegal moves

A document moves Draft → InReview → Published, or from review to ChangesRequested and back to Draft by editing. Fill in the TODOs:

  • step(doc, cmd) returns Moved(next) for a legal move and Rejected(doc), with the document unchanged, for anything else. A Draft accepts Edit and Submit; InReview accepts Approve and RequestChanges; ChangesRequested accepts only Edit; Published accepts nothing.
  • replay(start, cmds) applies the commands in order, skipping rejected ones, and returns the final document and how many were rejected.
