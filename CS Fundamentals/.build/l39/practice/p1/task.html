A price feed you can safely leave

PriceFeed holds a price and tells its subscribers about changes. Fill in the TODOs:

  • The AlertRule strategy decides whether a change is announced. The default, onChange, stays quiet when the price doesn't move. One check passes a rule of its own.
  • subscribe returns a handle. Closing it must end that subscription only, even when another listener is equal to it, or is the very same listener subscribed twice. Closing it again does nothing.
