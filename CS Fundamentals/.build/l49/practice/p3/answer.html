Why it works. Union by size keeps every tree at most lg n tall, and path compression flattens whatever find walks through, so later calls are nearly free. The naive quick-find is too slow for real use, but its answers are obviously right, and that makes it a good test oracle: 500 random operations agree with it.

Common mistake. Writing just parent[p] = root after the walk. It's still correct, but only p moves, and the nodes in between keep their long paths. The "every node on its path" check fails.
