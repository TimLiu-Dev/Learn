Dijkstra on numbered vertices

Write shortest(n, edges, source): the shortest distance from source to every vertex 0 until n, or null where no route exists. Edges are one-way and no weight is negative.

  • Use lazy Dijkstra, as in Part 2: a PriorityQueue (L20) of (distance, vertex), settle a vertex when it's polled, and skip stale entries.
  • The test graph has a cheap detour, a vertex that only has an edge out, and one with no edges at all.

The last check compares you with a Bellman–Ford oracle on 200 random graphs.
