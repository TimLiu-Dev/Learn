Why it works. The map and the doubly linked list (L17, L19) still give O(1) lookups and moves; only the eviction rule changed, from "while there are too many entries" to "while they weigh too much". Rejecting an oversized value and dropping its old copy keeps the cache from serving stale data, the Part 2 lesson again.

Common mistake. Evicting with if instead of while. With a count limit, one eviction always makes room for one entry, so if works; with weights, one heavy entry can need several light ones to leave, and the cache quietly ends up over its limit.
