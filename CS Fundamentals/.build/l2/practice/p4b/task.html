Read a chat command

A chat app reads commands such as /Mute @ada 10m. Fill in the TODOs:

  • command(input): the word right after a leading /, in lower case, ignoring spaces around the input. Not a command? Return null.
  • seconds(token): a duration like 45s, 10m or 2h in seconds. Anything else, such as 10min, gives null.

One check runs on a "Turkish laptop", so the answer mustn't depend on the default locale.
