Keep a HashMap from name to an entry (L19). Store the moment the answer expires, clock() + ttl, rather than the TTL itself: then the seconds left are just expiresAt - clock().

---

lookup has three outcomes: no entry (null), no time left (null, and you may as well remove the entry), or a Hit. "No time left" includes exactly 0.

---

For the JVM behaviour, one expression covers both caches: clock() + (fixedTtl ?: ttl). In Java, the clock is a LongSupplier and fixedTtl a nullable Long: fixedTtl != null ? fixedTtl : ttl.
