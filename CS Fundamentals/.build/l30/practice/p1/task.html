Is this address on my network?

192.168.1.0/24 is CIDR notation: a block of addresses whose first 24 bits are the same as 192.168.1.0. The /24 is the prefix length, and its mask is 24 ones then 8 zeros: ifconfig's netmask 0xffffff00. Fill in the TODOs:

  • parseIpv4 packs the four numbers into one 32-bit Int, the first number in the top 8 bits. Anything that isn't four numbers in 0–255 throws IllegalArgumentException.
  • maskOf(prefix) builds the mask, and cidrContains says whether an address falls inside a block. Bit masks and shifts are L48's.
