Why it works. Packing the octets makes an address one 32-bit number, so "the first 24 bits match" becomes a single and with the mask on each side. Routers do the same comparison to choose where a datagram goes next. Comparing with == after masking also sidesteps the sign: 192.168.1.151 is a negative Int, which doesn't matter to bit operations.

Common mistake. maskOf(prefix) = -1 shl (32 - prefix) with no special case. For /0 the shift distance is 32, the JVM keeps only its low 5 bits (JLS §15.19), and the mask comes out as all ones. Then 0.0.0.0/0, "every address", matches only 0.0.0.0.
