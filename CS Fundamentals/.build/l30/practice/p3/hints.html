frame is ByteBuffer.allocate(4 + n).putInt(n).put(bytes).array(), where bytes is the UTF-8 encoding and n is its size. A ByteBuffer is big-endian unless you change its order.

---

The decoder needs memory: a pending byte array that each feed appends to. Then loop: if at least 4 bytes are pending, read the length; if 4 + length bytes are pending, cut out one message and keep the rest. Otherwise stop and wait for the next chunk.

---

Inside the loop: ByteBuffer.wrap(pending, 0, 4).int reads the length, String(pending, 4, length, Charsets.UTF_8) is the message, and pending.copyOfRange(4 + length, pending.size) is what's left. Check for 4 bytes before reading the length: the prefix can be split too. In Java, join arrays with a ByteArrayOutputStream or Arrays.copyOf.
