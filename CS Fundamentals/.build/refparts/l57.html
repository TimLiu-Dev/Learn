Drift1 ppm = 86 ms/day; skew ≤ 2 × ppm × sync interval
Time-of-day ★currentTimeMillis, Instant.now(): NTP can step it, even backwards
MonotonicSystem.nanoTime(), TimeSource.Monotonic: durations and timeouts only
LWW ★safe only if writes are further apart than the skew; else the newer one is dropped
a → bsame process in order, or send → receive, or a chain of those
Lamporttick per event; on receive max(mine, msg) + 1
Lamport limita → b ⇒ L(a) < L(b), not the converse: can't spot concurrency
Vector clockone counter per node; receive = element-wise max, then tick own

Raft in brief

Rolesfollower → candidate → leader; a newer term makes anyone a follower
Electionno heartbeat for a random 150–300 ms: term++, vote self, RequestVote
One vote per term ★persisted with the term; a majority (3 of 5) wins
Why safetwo majorities always share a node, and it votes once
Up-to-datelater last term wins; same term → longer log wins
Replicationheartbeat = AppendEntries(prevIndex, prevTerm, entries, commit)
Committeda current-term entry stored on a majority; then apply in order
Accepted ≠ committed ★a minority leader accepts writes that are later dropped
TimingbroadcastTime ≪ electionTimeout ≪ MTBF
source: Raft paper (extended), §5 · raft.github.io
