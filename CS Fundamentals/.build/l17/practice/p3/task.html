Point at the broken bracket

An editor doesn't just say "unbalanced": it highlights the culprit. Write firstError(text), which looks only at ()[]{} and returns:

  • -1 if every bracket is matched;
  • the index of the first closer that has nothing open, or doesn't match the most recent opener;
  • otherwise, if openers are left over at the end, the index of the leftmost one that was never closed.
