The recursion is the problem: sumTo(1_000_000) needs a million frames on the stack at once. A for loop does the same additions inside one frame.

---

Keep a running total in a local variable and add each i from 1 to n. For a negative n, the range 1..n is simply empty.

---

Start the total at 0L, not 0: an Int total silently wraps past 2,147,483,647 (L2). In Java, that's long total = 0; and for (int i = 1; i <= n; i++).
