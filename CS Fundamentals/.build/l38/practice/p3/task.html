A fake that keeps the contract

HoldDesk depends on a ReservationStore. The real one uses SQL; write the in-memory FakeReservationStore the tests need. The interface's comments are the contract, and the hidden tests check every promise in them:

  • Ids are 1, 2, 3… across all books, and never reused, even after a cancel.
  • A queue is per book, oldest first, and a snapshot: adding later doesn't change a list you already returned.

The last check runs the real HoldDesk on your fake.
