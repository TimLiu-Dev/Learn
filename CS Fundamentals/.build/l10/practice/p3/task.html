A result type that doesn't hide bugs

readAge has an off-by-one bug, and its broad catch hides it: every row comes back as NotANumber. Fix it so it returns:

  • Ok(age) for "Ada,36" (spaces around fields are fine);
  • NotANumber(text) when the age field isn't a whole number;
  • Missing when there is no age field, or it's blank.

Catch only what you expect, or nothing at all.
