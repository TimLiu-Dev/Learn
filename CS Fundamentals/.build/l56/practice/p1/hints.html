An offset here is just a list position. After log += record, the new record's offset is log.lastIndex. For get, split the record the same way init does: split(" ", limit = 3) keeps a value with spaces in one piece.

---

A delete can't just forget the key in memory. Look at init: a reopen rebuilds the index from the log alone, so the log needs a "del key" record, or the old put comes back. Skip it when the key isn't there at all.

---

For compact, first collect each live key's newest record (index tells you where it is), then log.clear() and append them again, setting index[key] = log.lastIndex as you go. Clearing first would lose the records; keeping the old offsets would point past the end. In Java, the same steps use log.add and log.size() - 1.
