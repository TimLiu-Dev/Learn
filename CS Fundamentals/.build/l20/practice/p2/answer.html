Why it works. In-order visits left, node, right, so a BST's keys come out sorted, and the k-th one out is the k-th smallest. The sequence is lazy, so kthSmallest(root, 2) stops pulling after two keys. between is the same walk with pruning: when n.key ≤ lo, nothing on the left can be in range, so that whole subtree is skipped. That's what TreeMap.subMap does for you.

Common mistake. Writing elementAtOrNull(k). The question counts from 1 and the sequence counts from 0, so every answer is one place too far, and the 7th smallest comes back null.
