Balanced, and the floor below a key

insert and height are done. Write two more questions you can ask a BST:

  • isBalanced(root): true if, at every node, the heights of its left and right subtrees differ by at most 1. An empty tree is balanced.
  • floor(root, key): the largest key ≤ key, or null if there isn't one. Walk one path down, like contains.
