Why it works. eval is L20's post-order walk with arithmetic in place of println: a node can only compute once its children have. The environment is just a HashMap (L19), and the assignment evaluates its right side before it writes, so a let sees the old value. Int does the rest, including wrap-around (L2) and truncating division (L48).

Common mistake. Writing '/' -> a / b with no check. Division by zero then escapes as the JVM's ArithmeticException, with no Calc message, and callers that catch CalcError miss it.
