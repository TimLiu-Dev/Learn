Why it works. Precedence comes from who calls whom: expr only ever sees whole terms, so every * and / is finished before a + or - can use it. Associativity comes from the loop: each new Bin takes the tree so far as its left child, so equal operators group left to right.

Common mistake. Writing expr := term ( ("+" | "-") expr )?, recursion on the right. Precedence still works, but 8-3-2 becomes 8-(3-2) = 7. That's right for Calc's ^, and wrong for - and /.
