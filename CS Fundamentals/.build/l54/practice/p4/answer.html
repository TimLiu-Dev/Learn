Why it works. Post-order emission leaves both operands on the stack just before the instruction that needs them, so no instruction ever names its inputs. That's why the JVM's iadd has no operand either. The last test is a small differential test: two independent back ends, one answer.

Common mistake. Popping into a first, then b. Addition and multiplication don't care, so a quick test passes, but 8 - 3 becomes 3 - 8 = −5.
