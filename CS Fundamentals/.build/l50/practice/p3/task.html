Longest prefix match

A router holds a table of address prefixes and forwards each packet by the longest one that matches. Write two methods of PrefixTable:

  • insert(word), as in the lesson's trie.
  • longestPrefixOf(query): the longest inserted word that is a prefix of query, or null if there is none. With she and shells inserted, "shelf" gives "she".

Walk down once, at most query.length steps, remembering the last word end you passed.
