Announce a retirement in the headers

Exercise 5 hard-codes the headers. Write retirementHeaders(deprecatedAt, sunsetAt, docs), which builds them from two instants, so nobody has to work out Unix seconds and weekdays by hand:

  • Deprecation: @ plus the Unix seconds of deprecatedAt (RFC 9745).
  • Sunset: an IMF-fixdate such as Sun, 06 Nov 1994 08:49:37 GMT: English names, a two-digit day, always GMT (RFC 8594, RFC 9110 §5.6.7).
  • Link: <docs>; rel="deprecation".
  • A sunset earlier than the deprecation is a mistake: throw IllegalArgumentException. The same instant is allowed.
