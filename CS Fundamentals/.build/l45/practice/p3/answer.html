Why it works. The unit tests trust the fake to behave like SQLite, so the fake has to keep SQLite's promises, including the one the schema makes: one open loan per book. Running one contract suite against both stores (Part 3) is how you find out when they disagree.

Common mistake. Leaving the constraint out because "the service already checks openForBook". Then a service bug that skips the check passes every unit test, and only SQLite would refuse it, in production. That's the fake drifting from the real thing.
