Linear probing, by hand

ProbingMap is open addressing with a fixed number of slots: one key per slot, no chains. Fill in the TODOs:

  • put: start at the key's home slot, hashCode() floor-mod the capacity. Step forward one slot at a time, wrapping from the last slot to slot 0, until you find the key (replace its value) or an empty slot (store it there).
  • get: probe the same way. Reaching an empty slot means the key isn't here.
  • A full table must still answer get, and put of a new key throws IllegalStateException.
