Estimates as code, units included

Turn the Part 1 arithmetic into two functions a reviewer can test:

  • estimate(a) returns writes/s, reads/s, total storage in bytes after a.years, and cache bytes (the hot share of one month's new rows). A month is SECONDS_PER_MONTH.
  • minCodeLength(codes) returns the shortest base62 length that gives at least codes distinct codes. It must work for every positive Long, including 1 and Long.MAX_VALUE.

The checks use the shortener's numbers and the pastebin's from the final exercise.
