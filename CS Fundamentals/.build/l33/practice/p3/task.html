Deny by default

readNote returns a status and a body, like the lesson's handlers, but it lets anyone read anything. Make it follow the IDOR fix:

  • A missing or unknown token: 401 to "who are you?", checked before anything else.
  • A note that doesn't exist or isn't the caller's: 404 to "not found", so a stranger can't tell the two apart.
  • Only the owner gets 200 and the text.
