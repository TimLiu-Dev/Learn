Why it works. Everything needed to recompute the hash travels with it: algorithm, cost, salt. Re-hashing with exactly those and comparing with isEqual gives the answer in constant time, and every bad input ends in false, so a broken record can't crash a login or let it through.

Common mistake. Using a constant such as ITERATIONS instead of the stored count. It passes today, then every existing user is locked out the day you raise the constant. The stored count is what makes the upgrade-on-login exercise possible.
