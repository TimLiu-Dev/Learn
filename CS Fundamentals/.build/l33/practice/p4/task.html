Redact personal data from a log line

redact runs two patterns over a log line. Fix the first and write the second:

  • EMAIL is a too-greedy first attempt. An address must become [email], and punctuation after it, like a comma, must survive.
  • CARD: 13 to 19 digits, optionally split by single spaces or hyphens, becomes [card ****1111], keeping only the last four digits.
  • Order numbers, dates, versions, a stray @ and longer digit runs such as trace ids must be left alone.
