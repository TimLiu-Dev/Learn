The simulation needs three kinds of state: the shared count, and for each thread a register and a step number (0, 1 or 2). Two-element arrays work well, indexed by who - 'A'.

---

For each letter, look at that thread's step: 0 copies count into its register, 1 adds 1 to its register, 2 copies its register into count. Then move that thread on to its next step.

---

For allSchedules: with nothing left, the only schedule is "". Otherwise put "A" in front of every schedule of (a - 1, b) when a > 0, and "B" in front of every schedule of (a, b - 1) when b > 0 (L18's recursion). In Java, "A" + s does the same.
