Resolve versions the way Gradle does

Versions here are plain MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, and coordinates are group:name:version. Write three functions:

  • compareVersions(a, b): negative, zero or positive, comparing the numbers, not the text;
  • resolve(requests): for each module (group:name), the highest requested version wins, as in Gradle's default;
  • isBreaking(from, to): may this upgrade break callers, under the SemVer rules?
