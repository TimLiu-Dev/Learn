A fraction that is always in lowest terms

Make every Fraction valid and simplified the moment it exists:

  • Fraction(6, 8) stores num = 3, den = 4. Fraction(5) is 5/1. The sign lives in num, so den is always positive.
  • A zero denominator throws IllegalArgumentException. That includes Fraction(0, 0).
  • The secondary constructor turns "3/4" into a fraction by delegating to the primary one.
