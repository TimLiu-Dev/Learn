A battery that can't hold the wrong charge

Finish Battery using the property tools from this part:

  • level is clamped into 0..capacity on every write, including the starting value: Battery(100, 250) starts at 100.
  • percent is computed on every read: level × 100 / capacity, in whole numbers.
  • use(amount) drains the battery through the same rule. recharge() fills it and adds 1 to recharges, which outsiders can read but never assign.
