Why it works. The companion's cache exists once for the whole class, not once per instance, so every call to of sees the same map. Because the constructor is private, of is the only way in, so no second EUR object can ever be made. That's why === works for currencies here, like the Integer cache from L5, but for every value instead of just −128..127.

Common mistake. Validating and counting in of but still returning Currency(c) every time. Each call makes a new object, so of("usd") === of("USD") is false, and known counts calls, not currencies.
