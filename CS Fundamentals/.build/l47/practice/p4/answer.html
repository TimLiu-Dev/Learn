Why it works. Placing sheets in priority order means a sheet that spills over still gets the earliest free slot, ahead of anything due later. That's why d sits before a on day two. The require isn't decoration: with perDay = 0, every day is "full" and the while loop never ends.

Common mistake. Sorting by name alone. Then algebra takes today's only slot and zebra-tree, already two days overdue, waits another day. A sort with two keys (L14) states the priority exactly.
