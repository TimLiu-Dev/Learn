A review plan with a daily limit

After a bad week, Review can leave ten sheets due on one day. Write plan(sheets, today, perDay), which spreads them out:

  • Take sheets in order of due date (the most overdue first), then by name. An overdue sheet is due today.
  • Put each on its due day, or on the first later day with room: never more than perDay on one day.
  • Return the days in date order, each with its sheet names in the order they were placed. perDay below 1 is an IllegalArgumentException.
