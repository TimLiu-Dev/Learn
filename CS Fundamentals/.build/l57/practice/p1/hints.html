Which clock does startedAt read? Every duration in this class should start and end on the same clock, and only one of the two clocks never jumps.

---

Record monoNanos() when the lease starts. Elapsed time is monoNanos() - start, in nanoseconds, so divide by 1_000_000 before subtracting it from durationMs.

---

expiresAtWall() is wallMillis() + remainingMs(): the time of day comes from the wall clock, the amount left from the monotonic one. In Java, the clocks are LongSuppliers, read with getAsLong().
