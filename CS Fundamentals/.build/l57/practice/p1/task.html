A lease that NTP can't stretch

A leader holds a lease: "I may act as leader for the next 10 s." This one measures its time with the time-of-day clock, so an NTP step stretches or shortens it, just like Part 1's 5 s timeout.

  • Make remainingMs() and isValid() use the monotonic clock. It counts in nanoseconds; the lease works in milliseconds.
  • Fill in expiresAtWall(), the time of day printed in a log line: the wall clock's current reading plus the time left.
