Why it works. The coordinator may abort on its own any time before it decides, because no participant can have been told COMMIT yet. A timeout is therefore a safe reason to abort. Once it has logged a decision, though, it must stick to it: participants may already be acting on it. Counting participants in a set, not messages, makes a duplicated vote harmless, the same idea as L42's idempotent consumer.

Common mistake. A counter, if (++yesCount == participants.size). When orders-db's yes arrives twice, the counter reaches 2 and the coordinator commits while stock-db has never voted, and might be about to say no.
