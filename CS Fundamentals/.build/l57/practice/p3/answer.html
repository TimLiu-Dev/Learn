Why it works. Each line is one of Raft's rules. A stale term is refused, and a newer one is adopted first, whatever the vote turns out to be: that's how a node with a stale log still learns that the cluster has moved on. votedFor gives one vote per term, and a repeat request from the same candidate gets the same answer. The up-to-date check is the election restriction: a log whose last entry has a later term has heard from a newer leader, however short it is.

Common mistake. Comparing lengths first. A long log full of entries from an old term, which a minority leader accepted but never committed, would then win votes over a shorter log that holds a newer committed entry. That's the final's x=3 trying to come back.
