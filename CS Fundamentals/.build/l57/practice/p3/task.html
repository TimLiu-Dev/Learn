Should I vote for you?

Write Raft's RequestVote rule as a pure function: it gets what a voter remembers and a request, and returns the voter afterwards plus whether it granted the vote.

  • A request from an older term is refused. A request from a newer term makes the voter adopt that term with an unused vote, even if it then refuses.
  • Grant only if the voter hasn't voted for someone else this term, and the candidate's log is at least as up-to-date as its own (§5.4.1: compare last terms first; only on a tie, compare lengths).
