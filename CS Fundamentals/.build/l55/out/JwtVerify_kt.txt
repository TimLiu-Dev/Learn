genuine token              -> Valid(subject=ana)
unsigned (alg none)        -> Rejected(reason=alg must be HS256)
other algorithm            -> Rejected(reason=alg must be HS256)
signed by another key      -> Rejected(reason=bad signature)
expired 10 min ago         -> Rejected(reason=expired)
expired 30 s ago (leeway)  -> Valid(subject=ana)
for another service        -> Rejected(reason=wrong audience)
from another issuer        -> Rejected(reason=wrong issuer)
