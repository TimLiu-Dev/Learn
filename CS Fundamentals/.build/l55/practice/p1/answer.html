Why it works. The gate is an allow-list twice over: three named methods are safe, and one exact origin plus one exact token may do anything else. Everything the code doesn't recognise, whether a lookalike host, Origin: null or a new method, falls through to "reject". That's deny by default, applied to CSRF. MessageDigest.isEqual doesn't stop at the first differing byte, so response times don't leak how much of a guess was right.
