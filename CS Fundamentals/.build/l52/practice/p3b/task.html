Give the CPU a stack

The toy CPU below runs every Part 2 instruction. Fill in the two new ones, exactly as Part 4 describes:

  • CALL a: refuse with stack overflow at XX: SP=F0 when the stack is full, otherwise decrement sp, store the return address at mem[sp], and jump to a.
  • RET: refuse with stack underflow at XX when sp is 00, otherwise pop the return address into pc. XX is the address of the instruction, in hex; check(…) throws the IllegalStateException for you.
