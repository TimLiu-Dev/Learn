Why it works. ushr and and cut a byte into fields, and shl and or put them back. Because the fields don't overlap, or never disturbs a neighbour. The round-trip check confirms it for every instruction shape. This is Nand2Tetris's project 6 in miniature, and it makes the final's swapped-field bug impossible to type.

Common mistake. Putting rs in bits 2–3 and rd in bits 0–1. Programs that use only R0 still come out right, which hides it. SUB R2, R3 becomes 5E, which means SUB R3, R2, and LD R1, [80] becomes 21 80, which loads into R0.
