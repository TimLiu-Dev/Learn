Start with val t = tokens(line) and a when (t[0]) with one branch per mnemonic. There are only four shapes: nothing (HLT), a number (JMP), a register and a number (LDI, LD, ST, JZ), and two registers (ADD, SUB).

---

Two small helpers make every branch one line: "R2".removePrefix("R").toInt() gives 2, and "0E".toInt(16) gives 14. Then one packing function, encode(op, rd, rs, b1), builds intArrayOf(byte0, b1), with default arguments of 0.

---

Packing is decoding run backwards: (op shl 4) or (rd shl 2) or rs. If SUB R2, R3 gives 5E, your rd and rs have swapped places. In Java, use switch on the mnemonic, Integer.parseInt(t, 16), and op << 4 | rd << 2 | rs (shifts bind tighter than |).
