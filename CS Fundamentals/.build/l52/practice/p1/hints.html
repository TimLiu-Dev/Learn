Start with fullAdder. Two half adders: the first adds a and b (sum xor, carry and), the second adds that sum and carryIn. An or merges the two carries.

---

For addBits, keep one var carry = 0. Walk the indices from 0 up, call fullAdder(a[i], b[i], carry), append the sum bit, and set carry to the carry it returned.

---

After the loop, append carry once more: 15 + 1 needs 5 bits. Destructuring helps: val (s, c) = fullAdder(…). In Java, return a record SumCarry(int sum, int carry) as Adder.java does, and build a List<Integer>.
