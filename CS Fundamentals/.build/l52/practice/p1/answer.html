Why it works. Each column is one full adder, and its carry-out becomes the next column's carry-in: the wiring of the lesson's add8, for any width. The list grows by one bit because two n-bit numbers can add up to an (n + 1)-bit one. This full adder uses 4 + 2 + 4 + 2 + 3 = 15 nands, like the lesson's.

Common mistake. Returning after the loop without the last carry. Then 15 + 1 comes out as [0, 0, 0, 0]. That's L2's Int overflow: the JVM drops the carry by design, but here it's a bug.
