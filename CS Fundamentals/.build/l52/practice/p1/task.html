An adder of any width, from nand

The gates are given, wired from nand as in Gates.kt. Numbers are lists of bits, least significant bit first: 6 is [0, 1, 1].

  • Write fullAdder(a, b, carryIn) from the gates. It returns Pair(sum, carryOut).
  • Write addBits(a, b) for two lists of the same length n. Chain full adders from index 0 up, and return n + 1 bits: the last one is the final carry.

The checks compare your adder with + on thousands of inputs, and count your nand calls.
