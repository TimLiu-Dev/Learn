signMask: shr copies the sign bit as it shifts (L48), so shifting an Int right by 31 leaves 32 copies of it: all ones, or all zeros. For the pick, notice that b xor (a xor b) is a, and b xor 0 is b.

---

a < b exactly when a − b is negative, so signMask(a - b) looks like the mask you need. Then b xor ((a xor b) and mask) picks a when the mask is all ones. Run the checks: the small numbers pass, but the extremes don't.

---

Int.MAX_VALUE - (-1) overflows to Int.MIN_VALUE, a negative number, so the mask claims MAX < −1. Do the subtraction in a Long, where it can't overflow, and take its sign: ((a.toLong() - b) shr 63).toInt(). Java: (int) (((long) a - b) >> 63).
