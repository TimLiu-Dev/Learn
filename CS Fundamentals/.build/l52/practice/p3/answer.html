Why it works. The mask is either all ones or all zeros, so and mask keeps a xor b or wipes it, and the final xor turns that into a or b. Doing the subtraction as a Long gives the true sign of a − b for any two Ints. Part 3's loop could use x - 128 safely only because its values are 0–255.

Common mistake. signMask(a - b) with Ints. It's right whenever a − b fits, so small tests pass, but min(Int.MAX_VALUE, -1) returns Int.MAX_VALUE: the bit the JVM threw away (Part 1's carry) held the true sign.
