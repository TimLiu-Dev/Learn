Why it works. The list is private and every change goes through lend or giveBack, so the three rules are checked on every path. That's what makes Borrower an aggregate root rather than a bag of data: "Any references from outside the aggregate should only go to the aggregate root" (Fowler).

Common mistake. Making the field private but returning it from a getter typed List. Kotlin's read-only List is a view, not a copy: a cast gets the MutableList back, as L5's aliasing showed. Hand out a copy.
