Why it works. Each check matches a real failure from this part. The name check is why a valid certificate for example.com still fails for example.org. The date check is expired.badssl.com's CertificateExpiredException. The links and the trust store are the chain read bottom-up: here it ends at a cross-signed copy of the SSL.com root, issued by Comodo's AAA Certificate Services, which the JDK trusts.

Common mistake. Turning *.example.com into endsWith(".example.com"). Then api.v2.example.com passes, but RFC 9525 says a wildcard "can only match one label".
