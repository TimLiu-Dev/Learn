Handle the two special values first: a missing header means the client has nothing, and * means "whatever you have". Everything else is a list: split(","), then trim() each tag.

Weak comparison ignores the W/ prefix on either side, so compare tag.removePrefix("W/") with etag.removePrefix("W/"). The quotes are part of the tag, so v7 without quotes is not "v7".

The 304 is "HTTP/1.1 304 Not Modified\r\nETag: $etag\r\nCache-Control: $cacheControl\r\n\r\n": the final \r\n is the blank line, and nothing follows it. For the 200, body.encodeToByteArray().size is the length. In Java, that's body.getBytes(StandardCharsets.UTF_8).length.
