Be the origin from this part. Fill in:

  • clientHasIt(ifNoneMatch, etag): the header may be missing, *, or a comma-separated list of tags. Compare tags weakly, so W/"v7" matches "v7" (RFC 9110 §13.1.2).
  • respond(…): the whole response as it goes on the wire, every line ending in \r\n. A 200 sends the status line, ETag, Cache-Control, Content-Length, a blank line and the body. A 304 sends the status line, ETag, Cache-Control and the blank line, and nothing after it.
