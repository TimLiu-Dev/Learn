Clean up after a failed transfer

You're at the sqlite3 prompt, in the middle of the Part 1 trap. Inside an open transaction you logged a 150.00 transfer and credited Bob, and then Ada's debit failed the CHECK. Open "The tables you're querying" to see the session so far.

Ada really meant to send 50.00. Finish the session so that no trace of the failed transfer is left. Then move 5000 cents from Ada to Bob and log it in transfers, all as one transaction. The checker then lists every account and every logged transfer.
