The transaction from the setup is still open, and it contains two changes: the log row and Bob's credit. COMMIT would keep both. Which statement throws away everything since BEGIN?

---

Start with ROLLBACK;. Afterwards, BEGIN; a fresh transaction, insert the log row (1, 2, 5000), credit Bob, debit Ada, and COMMIT;.

---

The check expects Ada at 5000, Bob at 10000, and exactly one transfer, 1 -> 2 for 5000. If you see a second transfer row or Bob above 10000, something from the failed attempt was committed. In JDBC, the same ROLLBACK is the c.rollback() in the helper's catch.
