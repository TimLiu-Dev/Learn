Why it works. A failing statement only backs out itself; the transaction stays open with everything before it. ROLLBACK undoes the log row and Bob's credit in one move, because they were in the same transaction. The new transfer is then its own unit: all three statements commit together or not at all.

Common mistake. Committing first and undoing Bob's credit by hand with a reverse UPDATE. The balances come out right, but the log row for the failed 150.00 was committed too. Repairing by hand means remembering every change; ROLLBACK already does.
