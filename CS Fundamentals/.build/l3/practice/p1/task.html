Ticket prices and FizzBuzz

Fill in the three TODOs:

  • ticketPrice(age): under 3 is free (0), 3–12 pay 5, 13–64 pay 12, 65 and over pay 6. A negative age is invalid: return -1. Use when with ranges.
  • fizzBuzz(i): "Fizz" for multiples of 3, "Buzz" for multiples of 5, "FizzBuzz" for multiples of both, and otherwise the number as text.
  • fizzBuzzLine(n): the answers for 1 up to n, separated by single spaces, like "1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz". For 0 it's "".
