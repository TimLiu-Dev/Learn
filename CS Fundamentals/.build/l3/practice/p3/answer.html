Why it works. Every step that might have no value has a ? type, and the compiler won't let you skip handling it. ?: turns "no value" into an early return null, and ?.let runs the second lookup only when there's a boss to look up. The function's return type, String?, tells every caller that "nobody" is a possible answer.

Common mistake. bosses[bosses[name]!!]. It compiles and works for "ada", but throws a NullPointerException for anyone who isn't in the map. That's exactly the crash Kotlin was protecting you from.
