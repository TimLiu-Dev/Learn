Find the culprit in a trace

A trace is a list of spans; each knows its parent. A span's self time is its duration minus the time its children covered. Fill in the TODOs:

  • selfTimes maps every span id to its self time. Children can run in parallel and overlap: covered time counts once.
  • culprit returns the service of the span with the most self time.

Spans can arrive in any order, children before their parents.
