Coins that know their worth

Each Coin constant carries its value in cents. Fill in the two functions:

  • total(coins): the value of a handful of coins, in cents.
  • change(amount): the fewest coins that make amount, largest first. For 41 that's a quarter, a dime, a nickel and a penny.

Work from the constants' data (cents), not from the order they happen to be declared in.
