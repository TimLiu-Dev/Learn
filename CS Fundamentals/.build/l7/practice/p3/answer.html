Why it works. With the else gone, the compiler checks that describe covers every member of AgeInput. The next case someone adds, say TooYoung, becomes a compile error here instead of a wrong message. Inside each branch, the smart cast (Kotlin) or the pattern (Java) gives you that case's data with no cast.

Common mistake. Writing parseAge and leaving describe alone. Everything compiles, but OutOfRange(200) falls into the else, so someone who typed 200 is told to enter their age: PaymentsElse all over again.
