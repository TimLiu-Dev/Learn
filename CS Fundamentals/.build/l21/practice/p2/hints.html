It's Bfs.kt on a grid. Use an ArrayDeque as a FIFO queue, and a MutableMap<Cell, Int> that starts as mutableMapOf(start to 0). The map is also your visited set.

---

Take a cell from the front, then try its four neighbours. Skip one that's off the grid, a #, or already in the map. Otherwise its distance is the current cell's + 1: store it and add it to the back.

---

stepsTo is one line: distances(grid, start)[target] ?: -1. In Java, make Cell a record so it works as a HashMap key (L19), and use getOrDefault(target, -1).
