Why it works. Both styles solve each subproblem once. The table fills dp[0..n] in an order where every dp[i - s] is already done. The memo keeps the natural recursion but answers any repeated best(a) from the cache, so there are at most amount distinct calls, each trying every coin: O(amount × coins), the same as the table.

Common mistake. Greedy coin change: always take the biggest coin that fits. It's right for [1, 5, 10, 25], which is why it feels correct, but for 6 from [1, 3, 4] it takes 4 + 1 + 1, one coin more than 3 + 3.
