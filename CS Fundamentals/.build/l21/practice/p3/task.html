One table, one memo

Solve two problems, one in each DP style:

  • ways(n, steps), bottom-up: how many ways to climb n stairs when each move is one of the sizes in steps. Order matters, so 1 + 2 and 2 + 1 are different.
  • minCoins(coins, amount), top-down with a memo: the fewest coins that add up to amount, or -1 if it can't be done.

The final asks for coin change bottom-up, so this is the other half of the pair.
