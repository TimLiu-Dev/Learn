Why it works. If the count is about a·nᵇ, then count(2n) / count(n) ≈ 2ᵇ, and the a cancels. Counting steps gives the same ratios as a stopwatch, minus the JIT and the noise. n log n shows the honest catch: its ratios are 2.2, then 2.18, and they creep toward 2, so a doubling experiment can't easily tell it from linear. uniqueSlow comes out at 4: quadratic, with no timer involved.

Common mistake. log2(ratio).toInt(). It truncates, so a real-world quadratic ratio of 3.9 or 3.7 reads as linear. Round to the nearest whole number.
