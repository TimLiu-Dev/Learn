Don't wait for an exception: ZonedDateTime.of never throws for a missing or doubled time. Ask the zone instead. zone.rules.getValidOffsets(local) lists the offsets that local time can have.

---

An empty list means a gap: Skipped(local, ZonedDateTime.of(local, zone)), since of already shifts it forward. Two offsets mean an overlap: withEarlierOffsetAtOverlap() and withLaterOffsetAtOverlap() give both moments. One offset is the ordinary case.

---

For rfc3339, toString() is the problem: it leaves out :00 seconds. DateTimeFormatter.ISO_OFFSET_DATE_TIME.format(t.atZone(zone)) always writes them, and writes Z for UTC. Java's version is the same calls, with a switch on getValidOffsets(local).size().
