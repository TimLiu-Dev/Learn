Book a slot that may not exist

A booking form sends a local date-time and the library's zone. Before you store it, find out what that wall-clock time really means:

  • slot(local, zone) returns Exact(at) for a normal time, Skipped(requested, movedTo) in a spring-forward gap (with the time java.time would move it to), or Twice(earlier, later) in a fall-back overlap.
  • rfc3339(instant, zone) shows an instant in that zone as an RFC 3339 timestamp: seconds always included, and an offset (or Z) at the end.
