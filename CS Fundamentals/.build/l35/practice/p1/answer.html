Why it works. Each member's name and email now live in one row, so renaming Grace is a one-row update, and a member with no fines can exist. UNIQUE and NOT NULL turn the rules into something no program can skip, so the check's two bad inserts are ignored. The join on email gives each fine its surrogate member_id, and joining back loses nothing: all six fines return, unchanged.

Common mistake. Normalising the shape but leaving out UNIQUE. The tables look right and the round trip passes, but a second "Ada" with the same email slips in, and the next migration that joins on email doubles her fines. A schema that only looks normalised doesn't stop anomalies from coming back.
