Split the members out of the fines

fines_flat repeats each member's name and email in every fine: fine → member_email → member_name is a transitive dependency. Move it to 3NF:

  • Give members and fines the constraints they're missing: every member has an email, no two members share one, and every fine points at a real member.
  • Fill both tables with INSERT … SELECT from fines_flat. Each fine keeps its id.

The check then tries to sneak in a second Ada and a member with no email, and joins your tables back to compare them with the original.
