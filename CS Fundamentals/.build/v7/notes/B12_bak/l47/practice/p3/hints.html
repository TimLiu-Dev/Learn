First turn the text into (name, value) pairs: lines(), trim(), drop blanks and # comments, then lower-case the name so EXPIRES and Expires match. Then check Contact, then Expires.

---

Split each line at the first colon only: substringBefore(':') and substringAfter(':'). Values such as https://… and mailto:… contain colons of their own. Instant.parse reads the Expires value, and isBefore(now) tells you it's stale.

---

For Expires, a when with three cases: none, more than one, exactly one (then parse and compare). An Instant prints itself as 2025-12-31T18:37:07Z. Java: line.indexOf(':') and two substring calls, toLowerCase(Locale.ROOT).
