Why it works. RFC 9116 makes Contact and Expires the two required fields: Contact "MUST always be present", and Expires "MUST always be present and MUST NOT appear more than once". Field names are case-insensitive, and researchers "MUST ignore any fields they do not explicitly support", which is why Policy passes. A web Contact "MUST begin with https://".

Common mistake. line.split(":") and taking element 1. Contact: mailto:security@example.com becomes just mailto, and every URL becomes https. Split at the first colon only (L2's string work).
