A pool that makes you wait, but not forever

Shelf's pool waits in take() for as long as it takes. HikariCP gives up after a timeout instead. Build that rule on a fake clock (L38), so the tests can move time by hand.

  • borrow hands out a free connection, or puts the caller in line with a deadline.
  • release serves the longest waiter that is still waiting, or puts the connection back.
  • expire drops every waiter whose deadline has come. Each borrower picks its own timeout.
