The log records what happened, in order. Start with borrow: free.removeFirstOrNull() gives you a connection or null. Remember to add the connection to lent, so release can check it.

---

In release, refuse an unknown connection with check(lent.remove(conn)) { … }. Then call expire() before you look at the line: the clock may have moved without anyone calling advance. The first waiter left is the one to serve. If there is none, the connection goes back to free.

---

The line is ordered by arrival, not by deadline. A waiter with a 30 ms timeout can stand behind one with 100 ms, so expire must check every waiter, not just the first: val expired = waiters.filter { clock.now >= it.deadline }, then remove and log them. In Java, walk the deque with an Iterator and call it.remove().
