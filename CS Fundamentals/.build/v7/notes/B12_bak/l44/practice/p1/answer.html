Why it works. Each formula is a stated assumption times a unit conversion: per month to per second, years to months, one month's hot share to bytes. Counting the base62 digits of codes - 1 by division gives the exact length for every positive Long, because dividing can't overflow. That's the same digit loop as encode() in CodeGen.

Common mistake. Reusing the estimate's ceil(ln(codes) / ln(62)) as a general function. It's fine for 6 billion, but ln(1) = 0, so a single code gets length 0. The other trap is multiplying a Long up to 62^k: past 62^10 it overflows and wraps, and the loop can spin forever.
