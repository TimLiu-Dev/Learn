Spring AOP Proxies, Self-Invocation Trap & Filter Chains

Why did @Transactional silently fail to roll back your payment ledger update when called from another method in the same class? Because Spring annotations (@Transactional, @Async, @Cacheable) are literally the Proxy Pattern at runtime—and internal this.method() calls bypass the proxy completely!

5. 2027 Senior Backend Interview Corner

Q1: "Why does `@Transactional` on a `private` or `final` method fail in Spring Boot?"
Senior Answer: CGLIB generates a runtime subclass of your bean and overrides public/protected methods to wrap them in transaction logic. `private` and `final` methods cannot be overridden by a CGLIB subclass proxy, so Spring cannot intercept them!
