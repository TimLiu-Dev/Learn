Strategy & Policy Injection

PayVault calculates transfer fees differently for Standard ACH, Instant Card Payout, and VIP Zero-Fee tiers—and calculates retry delays differently under battery-saver vs. expedited sync. Strategy extracts interchangeable algorithms behind a clean interface or Kotlin fun interface so behavior swaps at runtime with zero when clutter.

1. Strategy in Modern Kotlin: fun interface

Per Refactoring.Guru, Strategy lets you define a family of algorithms and make them interchangeable. In Kotlin, SAM conversion via fun interface lets callers pass either a full class (when dependencies/state are needed) or a 1-line lambda!

5. 2027 Senior Android Interview Corner

Q1: "When is a Strategy class better than passing a raw lambda `(Long) -> Long`?"
Senior Answer: Use a named interface (`FeeStrategy`) or `fun interface` when the policy is part of your domain vocabulary, needs constructor dependencies (like an FX rate table), or is bound via DI (`@Binds`). Raw lambdas lose semantic meaning in multi-parameter constructors.
