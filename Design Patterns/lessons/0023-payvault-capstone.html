6. Resume Bullets & Senior Interview Defense

Copy-Ready Resume Project Entry PayVault — Offline-First Fintech Wallet & Modular Payment Engine (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, KMP, Room, WorkManager)
  • Architected a multi-module Clean/Hexagonal Android wallet with pure-Kotlin `:core:domain` isolation, achieving sub-second JVM unit tests for financial invariants and FSM transfer lifecycles.
  • Implemented a Transactional Outbox + persistent UUID Idempotency Keys + Full-Jitter exponential backoff, eliminating double-charges across dropped responses and offline transitions.
  • Built composable payment middleware (Decorator chains for auth/idempotency/metrics, Biometric Protection Proxy, and stateful Circuit Breaker isolation) with 3rd-party SDKs quarantined behind Anti-Corruption Adapters.
ADR Defense 1: "Walk me through what happens from the moment the user taps Pay in a subway tunnel until settlement."
Senior Walkthrough: 1) Compose emits `SubmitIntent` to ViewModel; MVI state moves to `Authorizing` (preventing double-tap). 2) Transfers > \$500 trigger `BiometricProxy`. 3) `CompliancePipeline` (Chain) validates KYC, balance, and daily cap locally. 4) `OutboxRepo` atomically writes a `PENDING` Ledger row + Outbox `TransferCommand` with a deterministic `Idempotency-Key` inside one Room `@Transaction`. 5) Room `Flow` updates UI immediately (`Pending Sync`). 6) When signal returns, `WorkManager` flushes the Outbox through a `CircuitBreaker` + `Idempotent` gateway; upon HTTP 200, Room updates to `SETTLED` and UI updates reactively.
ADR Defense 2: "If you had to cut scope for an MVP, which abstractions would you delay vs. keep from Day 1?"
Senior Walkthrough: Keep Day 1: `MoneyCents` value safety, Dependency Inversion around the 3rd-party payment SDK (Adapter), deterministic Transfer FSM, and Idempotency Keys (hard to retrofit after double-charges!). Delay until needed: Abstract Factory across multiple countries, PDF/JSON Visitors, and heavy multi-module Gradle splitting beyond 3–4 core layers.
