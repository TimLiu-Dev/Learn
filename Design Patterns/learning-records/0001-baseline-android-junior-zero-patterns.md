# Baseline: Junior Android Developer Starting Design Patterns from Zero

The learner is currently a junior Android developer targeting a 2027 Senior Android Engineer role, with zero prior formal knowledge of design patterns. Teaching must start from root principles (coupling, cohesion, composition vs. inheritance, SOLID) grounded in concrete Kotlin/Android pain points rather than abstract UML, building progressively toward the PayVault Offline-First Fintech Wallet capstone.

## Implications
- Do not assume familiarity with GoF terminology (Factory, Decorator, Strategy, etc.) until introduced and practiced in sequence.
- Anchor every abstraction to fast JVM unit testing, Jetpack Compose, or PayVault Fintech invariants so the learner sees *why* seniors care before learning *how* a pattern is wired.
