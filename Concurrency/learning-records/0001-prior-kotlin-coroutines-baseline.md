# Baseline: Junior Android Developer with Minimal Kotlin Coroutines

The learner is a junior Android developer targeting a senior concurrency skillset by 2027 and currently knows only a small amount of basic Kotlin Coroutines.

- **Evidence**: Disclosed directly by the learner during curriculum kickoff ("from a junior android developer in 2027, i barely know a little bit of kotlin coroutines thats all").
- **Implications**: Lessons start from hardware/OS fundamentals (cores, preemption, read-modify-write races) before unpacking JMM visibility, CPS state machines, structured concurrency, Flow internals, Android lifecycle/persistence concurrency, and distributed fencing tokens.
