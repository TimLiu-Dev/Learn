# Modern Android Development Resources

## Knowledge

- [Google Developers: Guide to App Architecture](https://developer.android.com/topic/architecture)
  The official, gold-standard architecture guide. Use for: UI layer, domain layer, data layer, offline-first guidelines, and UDF principles.
- [Google: Now in Android (GitHub Repository)](https://github.com/android/nowinandroid)
  Fully functional reference Android app demonstrating 100% modern best practices. Use for: multi-module design, convention plugins, and design systems.
- [Kotlin Documentation (Kotlinlang.org)](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/home.html)
  Official Kotlin language specification and standard library reference. Use for: Kotlin 2.x compiler, coroutines, context parameters, and serialization.
- [Jetpack Compose Official Documentation](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose)
  Primary reference for declarative UI. Use for: Composition lifecycle, side-effects, state hoisting, layout primitives, and animations.
- [Kotlin Coroutines & Flow Guide](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/coroutines-overview.html)
  Core concurrency reference. Use for: Structured concurrency, channels, cold and hot flows, and dispatchers.
- [Android Developers YouTube Channel & Android Backstage Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/@AndroidDevelopers)
  Official deep-dives from the Android engineering team at Google. Use for: System internals, runtime optimizations, and Compose internals.
- [ProAndroidDev & AndroidPub](https://proandroiddev.com)
  High-signal community articles by Google Developer Experts (GDEs) and staff engineers. Use for: Case studies, architectural benchmarks, and real-world migrations.
- [Android Developers: 16 KB Page Sizes & Adaptive Apps](https://developer.android.com/guide/practices/page-sizes)
  Official Android 15/16/17 compliance guides for 16 KB ELF alignment, `WindowSizeClass` resizability, and Predictive Back. Use for: Play Store compliance and multi-form-factor architecture.
- [Android Developers: Credential Manager & Play Integrity](https://developer.android.com/identity/sign-in/credential-manager)
  Primary guides for Passkeys, unified sign-in, and server-verified app/device attestation. Use for: Authentication and anti-abuse engineering.
- [Android Developers: Android Vitals & ApplicationExitInfo](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals)
  Official production health metrics, ANR/LMK/OOM exit reason diagnostics, and staged rollout playbooks. Use for: Production observability and incident triage.
- [JetBrains & Google: Kotlin Multiplatform & Compose Multiplatform](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/multiplatform.html)
  Official KMP documentation and Android Jetpack KMP library guides (Room KMP, DataStore, Ktor, Koin). Use for: Sharing business logic and UI natively across Android and iOS.
- [Android Developers: Gemini Nano & On-Device AI](https://developer.android.com/ai/gemini-nano)
  Official AICore and ML Kit GenAI documentation for local inference. Use for: Privacy-first, offline-capable AI features.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/androiddev](https://reddit.com/r/androiddev)
  Premier discussion hub for Android engineers. Use for: Industry trend analysis, hiring expectations, library critique, and technical debates.
- [Kotlinlang Public Slack (#android, #compose, #coroutines)](https://kotlinlang.slack.com)
  Official JetBrains & community Slack. Use for: Direct troubleshooting with library maintainers, compiler questions, and cutting-edge idiom discussions.
- [Android Worldwide Community](https://androidworldwide.com)
  Global network of Android user groups and tech meetups. Use for: Networking, conference talks, and open-source collaboration.
