Aether is an offline-first multi-asset portfolio and financial telemetry tracker. It is designed from day one to showcase senior-level engineering on your resume: multi-module, offline-first Room cache, real-time WebSocket streams, hardware biometrics, and 85%+ test coverage.

🌱 Junior Android Dev Mental Model: Aether Module Graph
:app -> :feature:market, :feature:portfolio, :feature:vault
:feature:* -> :core:data, :core:designsystem, :core:model
:core:data -> :core:database, :core:network
:core:database, :core:network -> :core:model (Pure Kotlin)
