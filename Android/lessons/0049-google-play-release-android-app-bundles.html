Google Play Release & Android App Bundles (AAB)

Prepare production Android App Bundles (.aab), configure Play App Signing, and monitor Android Vitals.

Recommended Primary Source
Google Android: About Android App Bundles
Official Documentation →

Google Play requires all applications to be published as Android App Bundles (.aab) rather than monolithic APKs. Google Play uses the AAB to generate and serve optimized split APKs tailored to each user's specific device configuration (CPU architecture, screen density, and language).

🌱 Junior Android Dev Mental Model: Android Vitals

Once your app is in production, Google monitors Android Vitals in the Play Console:

  • Crash Rate: Must remain well below 1.09% (bad behavior threshold).
  • ANR Rate: Application Not Responding rate must be under 0.47%. Exceeding this penalizes your app's search visibility in Google Play!
