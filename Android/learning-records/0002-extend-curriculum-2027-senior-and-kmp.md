# Curriculum Extension: 2027 Senior Frontier & KMP (Lessons 0061–0072)

Following an audit of `roadmap.sh/android` and 2027 Senior Android expectations, the learner elected to extend the curriculum to 72 comprehensive lessons incorporating Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP), brownfield interop, media pipelines, system surfaces, and production feature flagging. See [[MISSION.md]] for the updated scope.

## Implications
- Obsolete topics (Java, RxJava, `AsyncTask`, `EncryptedSharedPreferences`, Gson) remain excluded, while brownfield View/Fragment-to-Compose migration interop (`ComposeView`, `AndroidView`, `ViewCompositionStrategy`) is explicitly taught in Lesson `0069`.
- Phase IX (`0061`–`0072`) covers 2027-critical senior topics: Android 16/17 platform mandates (16 KB memory pages, mandatory large-screen resizability, Predictive Back), Credential Manager & Play Integrity, JVM screenshot testing, `ApplicationExitInfo` observability, KMP shared architecture (Ktor/Room KMP/Koin), Compose Multiplatform & On-Device AI (Gemini Nano), Brownfield Compose/View Interop, Jetpack Media3 (ExoPlayer) & CameraX, Jetpack Glance Widgets & Android 16/17 Live Updates, and Feature Flags & Analytics Architecture at scale.
