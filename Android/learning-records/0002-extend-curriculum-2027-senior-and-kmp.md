# Curriculum Extension: 2027 Senior Frontier & KMP (Lessons 0061–0068)

Following an audit of `roadmap.sh/android` against 2027 Senior Android expectations, the learner elected to extend the curriculum past 60 lessons and incorporate Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP). See [[MISSION.md]] for the updated scope.

## Implications
- Legacy `roadmap.sh/android` topics (Java, XML layouts, Fragments, MVP/MVC, RxJava, `SharedPreferences`, Espresso-first UI tests) remain excluded or strictly read-only interop.
- Phase IX (`0061`–`0068`) adds 2027-critical senior topics missing from both `roadmap.sh/android` and the original 60-lesson plan: Android 16/17 platform mandates (16 KB memory pages, mandatory large-screen resizability, Predictive Back), Credential Manager & Play Integrity, JVM screenshot testing, `ApplicationExitInfo` observability, KMP shared architecture (Ktor/Room KMP/Koin), Compose Multiplatform, and On-Device AI (Gemini Nano).
