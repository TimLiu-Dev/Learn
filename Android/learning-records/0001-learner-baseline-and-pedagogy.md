# Junior Android Developer Baseline & Pedagogical Alignment

The learner is a junior Android developer entering the industry, requiring deep grounding in modern 2026/2027 standards (Kotlin 2.x, Jetpack Compose, Navigation 3 & 2.8+ type-safe, UDF/MVI, Hilt, Room, and WorkManager). All lessons must emphasize clear mental models, contrast junior anti-patterns with production standards, and reinforce learning through runnable code snippets, in-browser practice with progressive hints, and code-reading bug hunt quizzes explaining why choices succeed or fail.
