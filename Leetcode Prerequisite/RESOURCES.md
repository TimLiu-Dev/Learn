# Algorithmic Thinking & Technical Interview Foundations Resources

## Knowledge

- [Big-O Cheat Sheet & Complexity Reference](https://www.bigocheatsheet.com/)
  Comprehensive reference for time and space complexities of data structures and algorithms. Use for: quick verification of standard structure operations.
- [Asymptotic Notation Guide — GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/analysis-of-algorithms-set-1-asymptotic-analysis/)
  Explanations of Big O, Big Omega, and Big Theta. Use for: formal complexity bounds definitions.
- [Competitive Programmer's Handbook (Antti Laaksonen) – Chapter 2 (Time Complexity)](https://cses.fi/book/book.pdf)
  Clear, operational explanation of time complexity, the 10⁷ ops/sec rule, and loop analysis. Use for: constraint math and runtime bounds.
- [Kotlin Standard Library Collections & Array Documentation](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/collections-overview.html)
  Official reference for `IntArray`, `ArrayList`, `ArrayDeque`, `HashMap`, and `HashSet`. Use for: idiomatic, zero-overhead Kotlin collection selection.
- [Open Data Structures (Pat Morin) – Chapters on Array-Based Lists & Hash Tables](https://opendatastructures.org/)
  Mathematical proof of geometric doubling amortized O(1) runtime and separate-chaining collision bounds. Use for: deep CS hardware and amortized analysis proofs.
- [Google Research: Nearly All Binary Searches and Mergesorts are Broken (Joshua Bloch)](https://ai.googleblog.com/2006/06/extra-extra-read-all-about-it-nearly.html)
  Seminal breakdown of 32-bit signed integer overflow in `(low + high) / 2` and the `low + (high - low) / 2` fix. Use for: boundary analysis and numeric safety.
- [Tech Interview Handbook – Coding Interview Best Practices](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-prep/)
  Pragmatic rubric on communication, problem decomposition, and whiteboard conduct. Use for: interview protocol scripts and cadence.
- [Grind 75 & Blind 75 Curated Study Plans](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/)
  Curated collection of highest-yield LeetCode problems grouped by algorithmic pattern and difficulty. Use for: structured post-foundation problem solving roadmap.

## Wisdom (Communities & Visualizers)

- [PythonTutor / Code Execution Visualizer](https://pythontutor.com/)
  Interactive line-by-line frame-by-frame call stack and heap visualization tool. Use for: dry-running recursive stack frames and pointer traversals.
- [NeetCode Community & Video Breakdowns](https://neetcode.io/)
  Visual, step-by-step algorithmic patterns and interview communication walkthroughs. Use for: pattern recognition and problem walkthrough models.
- [LeetCode Discuss (General Discussion & Interview Experiences)](https://leetcode.com/discuss/general-discussion)
  Real-world candidate reports and contemporary interview formats. Use for: calibrated expectations of current hiring rubrics.
