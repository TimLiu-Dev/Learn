# Prior Baseline: Junior Android Developer Targeting Senior DevOps (2027)

The learner enters with junior Android development experience (Android Studio, Gradle builds, `adb`, keystores) and aims to reach Senior DevOps Engineer by 2027 via a two-stage path: first Senior Android/Spring Boot app-lifecycle DevOps, then cloud/Kubernetes/GitOps/SRE platform engineering.

- **Implications**: Anchor early infrastructure lessons in Gradle, JVM/Spring Boot processes, signing keystores, and `adb shell` parallels rather than assuming prior sysadmin experience.
