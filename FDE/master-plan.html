1. FDE 101 & Prerequisites (Read Before Lesson 0001)

If you have never worked as or alongside a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), start here. Pioneered at Palantir ("Deltas") and now central at frontier AI labs and enterprise platforms, an FDE is a high-agency software engineer embedded directly with enterprise customers to integrate, harden, and deploy working production systems inside messy real-world infrastructure (Palantir Blog; The Pragmatic Engineer).

What an FDE Does Weekly

  • 15–20% Discovery: Shadow customer operators & security teams to isolate the real bottleneck and lock a bounded Statement of Work (SOW).
  • 40% Data & Infra Plumbing: Build Python/TS connectors, webhooks, SQL pipelines, SSO/ACL rules, and Docker/K8s configs.
  • 25% Applied AI & UI: Ship Hybrid RAG, Text2SQL, MCP tools, React operator consoles, and evaluation suites.
  • 15% Product Flywheel: Convert recurring field workarounds into declarative configs and core product RFCs.

What You Already Bring (Android)

  • Typed OOP thinking (Kotlin/Java) that maps cleanly to TypeScript & Python Pydantic models.
  • REST/JSON integration experience (Retrofit) & explicit async concurrency (Coroutines).
  • Defensive UI state instincts (StateFlow / Compose) & Git collaboration workflows.
  • Healthy paranoia about flaky networks and strict device latency/memory budgets.

Prerequisites You Bridge in Track 1

  • Python & TypeScript reading/writing: Covered immediately in Unit 0000.
  • Practical Graph/DAG DSA: BFS/DFS, topological sort, intervals (Unit 0004).
  • SQL & Postgres internals: Window audits, B-tree/GIN/HNSW indexes, keyset paging (0008–0009).
  • VPCs, TLS, OIDC & Docker/K8s: Subnets, PrivateLink, IAM, container triage (0010–0012).

Why Split Into Mid-Level First, Then Senior?

Mid-Level FDE (2–5 yrs SWE equiv.) is the mainstream hiring sweet spot for startups and scaling platforms—and is enough to land high-paying roles in 2027 while getting real embedded production experience. Track 1 gets you job-ready and applying fast with AegisOps Core; Track 2 adds the enterprise ReBAC, Text2SQL, VPC/vLLM, MicroVM/MCP/A2A, and calibrated eval gates that Senior and Frontier Lab loops test.

How to Spot "Fake FDE" Job Postings

Ask interviewers: (1) "What % of my week is writing production code in Git?" (look for 70%+), (2) "Give an example where an FDE field fix became a core product feature", and (3) "Are FDEs evaluated on shipped customer outcomes or hourly billable utilization?"
