1. How Top Companies Grade Senior FDE Candidates in 2027

Unlike pure LeetCode SWE loops, FDE interviews evaluate your chaos ceiling across four rounds (Palantir Blog; Pragmatic Engineer):

  1. Discovery / Decomp Round: Unpack ambiguity in minutes 0–10 before drawing boxes; isolate buyer vs. operator KPIs, data owners, CISO blockers, and a Week-1 thin slice.
  2. Practical Production Coding: Clean, edge-case-hardened connectors, rate-limiters, deduplication, SSE parsers, or typed tool dispatchers—with zero unhandled nulls or silent corruption.
  3. Applied AI & Enterprise System Design: Pre-filter ACLs (never post-filter!), Contextual Hybrid Search (BM25+RRF), MicroVM sandboxes for agent code, OTel GenAI spans, and calibrated eval CI gates.
  4. Productization & Android Narrative: Frame your Android background as an asset (hostile mobile networks, defensive parsing, strict UI state) plus proof from your AegisOps capstone!
Your Android Pitch:
"Building mobile clients taught me to assume networks flake and inputs lie. Over the last months I extended that rigor to backend pipelines, enterprise ACLs, hybrid RAG, and eval-gated agents."
