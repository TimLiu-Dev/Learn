1. Hosting Decisions: Economics + Egress Reality

Enterprise buyers constantly ask Senior FDEs: "Should we self-host an open-weight model on GPUs in our AWS VPC or call Claude/GPT over PrivateLink?" Guessing violates engineering rigor (roadmap.sh FDE; vLLM Docs). Evaluate three dimensions:

  1. Utilization Math: A self-hosted vLLM GPU node bills 24/7 fixed hourly cost even at 3% traffic (e.g., $2.50/hr × 730h = $1,825/mo), whereas serverless Cloud APIs bill strictly per million tokens. Low/spiky volume heavily favors Cloud APIs or Bedrock/Vertex private VPC endpoints; sustained high-throughput classification favors self-hosted vLLM.
  2. Air-Gapped / Restricted Egress: Defense and regulated banking enclaves prohibit public internet egress outright—requiring private registries, local embedding models, and VPC-hosted vLLM.
  3. Tiered Model Routing: Route fast classification/extraction to small 8B local models (< 250ms) and reserve frontier reasoning models for complex synthesis.
Rule of Thumb:
Never self-host vLLM just "because it feels cheaper"—at low utilization, idle GPU hours cost 5x–10x more than API token billing!
