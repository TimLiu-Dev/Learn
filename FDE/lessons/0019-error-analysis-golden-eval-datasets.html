Error Analysis & Golden Datasets from Real Logs

Replacing "looks good to me" prompt vibes with Hamel Husain's failure-driven workflow: reading real traces, axial coding failure modes, prioritizing by Pareto impact, and curating golden regression sets.

Estimated Time: 25 min Prerequisites: 0016, 0018 Tangible Win: Build a Trace Failure Pareto Analyzer & Stratified Golden Set Curator

1. Why Generic Benchmarks Lie to Enterprise FDEs

Generic model leaderboards measure high-school math or TriviaQA—zero correlation with whether your copilot parses Acme's messy shipping codes without leaking permissions. As Hamel Husain emphasizes in Your AI Product Needs Evals, high-performing AI teams start by manually reading 50–100 real production traces:

  1. Open Coding → Axial Taxonomy: Jot down specific failure notes on every bad trace, then group them into crisp binary tags (missed_sku_bm25, fabricated_citation, ignored_currency_unit, over_eager_refusal).
  2. Pareto Prioritization: Fix the #1 or #2 failure buckets accounting for 70% of real customer pain instead of tuning fancy agent prompts for a 1% edge case.
  3. Golden Regression Dataset: Freeze sanitized real failures (never synthetic-only happy paths!) balanced across workflows and customer tiers so every fix stays fixed forever.
Eval Law #1:
Spend 30 minutes looking at real trace data before writing a single LLM-as-judge prompt—otherwise you automate grading for the wrong bug!
