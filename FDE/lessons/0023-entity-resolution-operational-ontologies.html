Entity Resolution & Operational Ontologies

Reconciling fragmented CRM, SAP, and support records into one canonical business entity graph—and exposing typed ontology objects and safe actions for both humans and AI agents.

Estimated Time: 25 min Prerequisites: 0007 Tangible Win: Implement Transitive Entity Clustering & Ontology Link Validator

1. Why Raw Tables Break AI Agents & Dashboards

Enterprise organizations rarely share one clean foreign key. In Salesforce the client is acct_91 ("Acme Corp.", billing@acme.io); in SAP they are CUST-0091 ("ACME CORPORATION", Tax ID 94-12345); in Zendesk they are org_881 (Tax ID 94-12345, ops@acme.io).

Without Entity Resolution, neither a human analyst nor an AI copilot knows these three records represent one customer. Palantir popularized modeling reconciled entities inside an Operational Ontology (Palantir Blog):

  1. Deterministic Blocking Keys First: Match on high-precision normalized identifiers (Tax ID, canonical domain+email, verified ERP ID)—never auto-merge two companies on fuzzy string name alone!
  2. Transitive Graph Clustering (Union-Find): If Record A shares taxId with B, and B shares email with C, all three belong to one canonical cluster with explicit sourceRefs provenance.
  3. Typed Ontology + Action Links: Bind entities (Account, Order, Ticket) with typed edges and surface dangling foreign keys cleanly rather than dropping rows silently.
Avoid False Merges:
Splitting an unmerged duplicate later is annoying; unmerging two distinct clients falsely fused together (corrupting invoices & ACLs!) is a disaster. Optimize for high precision first.

2. Annotated Code: Python Entity Provenance & Canonical Survivorship

Python Keeping Source-System Provenance Inside Canonical Entities 
canonical_account = {
    "canonical_id": "ent_001",
    "display_name": "Acme Corporation",  # Chosen from highest-trust source (SAP > CRM)
    "source_refs": [
        {"system": "salesforce", "id": "acct_91"},
        {"system": "sap", "id": "CUST-0091"},
        {"system": "zendesk", "id": "org_881"}
    ]
}
