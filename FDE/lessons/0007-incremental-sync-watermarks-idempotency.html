Incremental Sync, Watermarks & Idempotent Upserts

Moving past brittle full-table nightly dumps: cursor checkpoints, late-arriving lookback windows, and monotonic versioned upserts that survive out-of-order replays cleanly.

Estimated Time: 25 min Prerequisites: 0005, 0006 Tangible Win: Build a Lookback Watermark Planner & Monotonic Upsert Store

1. Why Full Re-Syncs and Naive > watermark Queries Fail

On Android Room caches, you often wipe-and-replace a small list of 50 items. In enterprise integrations with 20 million rows, wiping and re-ingesting every night saturates API quotas and breaks real-time SLAs.

Yet naive incremental syncs introduce three silent data-loss bugs:

  1. Boundary Tie Loss: If rows share the exact same millisecond updated_at, querying strictly updated_at > last_watermark drops sibling rows on the next run; querying >= with a tiny lookback overlap window plus idempotent upserts catches them safely.
  2. Late-Commit / Clock Skew Gaps: Long-running DB transactions in customer ERPs commit row T = 10:00:01 at 10:00:09 after your 10:00:05 poll already advanced the watermark! A lookback buffer (e.g., 5 min) closes the gap.
  3. Out-of-Order Overwrites: A delayed retry of v2 arriving after v3 will overwrite newer state unless your upsert enforces a monotonic guard (WHERE excluded.updated_at > target.updated_at).
Golden Rule of Checkpoints:
Advance the saved watermark only after the batch transaction commits durably—never before writing!

2. Annotated Code: Postgres Monotonic Idempotent Upsert

SQL / Postgres Out-of-Order Safe Idempotent Upsert 
INSERT INTO orders (tenant_id, order_id, status, amount, source_updated_at)
VALUES ($1, $2, $3, $4, $5)
ON CONFLICT (tenant_id, order_id)
DO UPDATE SET
  status = EXCLUDED.status,
  amount = EXCLUDED.amount,
  source_updated_at = EXCLUDED.source_updated_at
-- Monotonic guard: stale retries NEVER overwrite newer state!
WHERE EXCLUDED.source_updated_at > orders.source_updated_at;
