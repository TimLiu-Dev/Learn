Dataset Capture Manual & Hardware Matrix

Exactly what photos and videos to shoot of yourself and your cat—angles, outfits, full-body framing, lighting, and lens rules—plus how to split workloads across your M5 Max (128GB), ROG Flow Z13 (128GB), and rented cloud GPUs.

1. Your Hardware Playbook: Local 128GB vs. Cloud GPUs

Owning two 128GB unified-memory machines eliminates the #1 bottleneck most hobbyists hit (running out of 16GB–24GB VRAM). You can load massive 14B–32B models locally and privately—and save rented NVIDIA cloud GPUs strictly for high-speed LoRA training and heavy video batches.

Machine / Tier Sweet-Spot Workload Critical Setup Rules (Late 2026)
MacBook Pro M5 Max (128GB UMA) Private FLUX.2 / SDXL photo workflows, ComfyUI graph prototyping, short Wan 2.2 I2V clips Set export PYTORCH_MPS_HIGH_WATERMARK_RATIO=0.0.
Use GGUF (Q4_K_M, Q5_K_M, Q8_0) or native BF16 (M5 Max has hardware BF16; avoid blind --force-fp16 on FLUX/Wan and avoid FP8 on MPS).
Disable BlockSwap (your RAM is VRAM). Use step-distilled LoRAs (LightX2V / Wan-Lightning) for 4–6 step Wan 2.2 previews.
ROG Flow Z13 (Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Strix Halo, 128GB) Heavy local ComfyUI pipelines, private/uncensored SDXL/Pony/FLUX/Wan runs Carve 96GB–112GB VRAM in BIOS / AMD Adrenalin.
Run native Windows/Linux ROCm 7.x (gfx1151 / Strix Halo ComfyUI builds) with FP8 or GGUF weights.
Cloud GPU Rental (RunPod L40S 48GB ~$0.99/hr, H100, Vast.ai) Fast LoRA training (ai-toolkit / kohya_ss) & multi-shot Wan 2.2 14B video batches Unified memory has huge capacity, but discrete datacenter GPUs have far higher compute & bandwidth for backprop: a FLUX LoRA finishes in ~20–35 min (~$0.50–$1.50 total).

2. Shooting Your Selfies & Body Dataset (20–25 Photos)

Why 20–25? Modern FLUX/SDXL trainers learn from diversity, not spam. 22 sharp, varied photos beat 80 repetitive mirror selfies every time. This single curated dataset powers your LinkedIn headshots, Tinder dating photos, and (if you include optional body-proportion shots) consistent torso/anatomy in private local workflows.

A. Exact Framing Breakdown (Aim for 22 Photos Total)

Shot Type Count Exact Pose & Angle Checklist
Close-Up Portraits (Head & Shoulders) 8–10 • 2 straight-on eye-level (1 neutral/confident for LinkedIn, 1 warm smile with teeth).
• 3 turned 30°–45° left & right (looking at camera & looking slightly off-camera).
• 2 true side/3-quarter profiles (teaches jawline, nose bridge, ear placement).
• 2 relaxed candid expressions (slight laugh, closed-mouth half-smile, serious focus).
Medium / 3/4 Shots (Waist or Thigh-Up) 6–7 • Standing straight-on and angled 30°.
• Seated at a cafe table or desk (hands resting naturally—teaches hand scale & posture).
• Walking toward camera or leaning casually with arms relaxed (avoid crossing arms tightly in every shot).
Full-Body Shots (Head to Toe) 5–6 • Standing relaxed front-facing (include shoes & full legs—never crop at ankles!).
• Standing 45° side angle & mid-stride walking.
• Sitting full-body on steps/bench.
• Crucial: Without full-body shots, LoRAs guess your height-to-shoulder ratio and ruin Tinder lifestyle frames.

B. What to Wear & Grooming Rules

  • Rotate 5–7 Plain, Well-Fitting Outfits: Mix plain crew-neck t-shirts (black, white, olive/navy), a crisp collared shirt or casual button-down, a fitted sweater/jacket, and clean jeans/chinos. Never wear the same shirt in more than 2–3 photos or the model will permanently glue that collar to your neck.
  • Avoid Busy Logos & Graphics: Giant brand text or loud patterns confuse captioning and leak random text onto generated LinkedIn suits.
  • Zero Sunglasses, Hats, or AirPods: Keep forehead, hairline, ears, and eye spacing 100% unobstructed.
  • Glasses Rule: If you never wear glasses on LinkedIn/Tinder, take all photos without them. If you wear glasses sometimes, shoot 75% without and 25% with thin frames so the LoRA supports both.
  • Optional Body-Proportion / NSFW Prep (Private Local Only): If you plan to generate beach/fitness Tinder photos or private uncensored images locally, include 3–5 well-lit, timer-shot shirtless or fitted-swimwear/underwear photos (chest-up + full-body, no phone covering your face in a mirror). Otherwise the base model invents an unrelated torso.

C. Camera, Lens & Lighting Checklist

Three Photo Traps That Ruin LoRAs
  1. Front Selfie Cam Wide-Angle Distortion: Holding your phone arm's-length with the 24mm front cam stretches your nose and shrinks your ears. Use the rear 1x (or 2x/50mm) lens on a cheap tripod/shelf with a 3–10s timer, or have a friend shoot from 5–8 feet away.
  2. Apple Portrait Mode / Beauty Smoothing: Turn OFF artificial Portrait bokeh and skin-retouching filters. Synthetic blur edges and smoothed pores teach the LoRA "plastic AI skin."
  3. Same Room, Same Lamp: Shoot across at least 4–5 locations & lighting moods—facing an open window (soft natural daylight), outdoor open shade, overcast street/park, golden hour side light, and warm indoor ambient light.

3. Shooting Your Cat Dataset (15–25 Photos + 3–5 Clips)

Cats are harder than human faces because fur patterns (especially tabby stripes, calico patches, or tuxedo bibs) shift rapidly if you only capture top-down phone angles.

Cat Shot Type Count What It Teaches the Model
Face & Eye Close-Ups 6–8 Exact eye color, nose leather tint, whisker pad, ear tufts, and forehead markings (shoot straight-on, 45°, and side profile at cat eye-level—not standing over the cat looking down!).
Medium Lifestyle Shots 6–8 Loafing on a couch, curled in a bed, sitting upright by a window, perched on a cat tree. Capture sleepy half-closed eyes AND wide-eyed toy-focus alertness.
Full-Body & Action 5–7 Side profile standing (shows full tail length, leg proportions, and flank markings), stretching, walking, mid-play/pouncing. Bright room so paws aren't motion-blurred!
Short Reference Videos (3–5 sec, 1080p) 3–5 clips Steady, tripod/braced clips: (1) cat blinking/head-tilt, (2) walking across frame left-to-right, (3) batting a wand toy. Used as multimodal refs in Seedance 2.5 and motion studies for Wan 2.2.

4. How to Organize Your Files Now

datasets/
├── me_raw/          # 20-25 original high-res JPG/PNGs (do NOT square-crop!)
├── me_body_opt/     # Optional 3-5 clean physique/swimwear refs (kept local only)
├── cat_raw/         # 15-25 sharp cat photos across close-up, medium, full-body
└── cat_video_refs/  # 3-5 steady 3-5s MP4 clips at 1080p

Leave original aspect ratios (3:4, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1) intact—modern trainers like ai-toolkit automatically bucket mixed aspect ratios around 1 megapixel (1024×1024 / 896×1152).
