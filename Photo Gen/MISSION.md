# Mission: Senior AI Photo & Video Generation Engineer (2027)

## Why
Transition from a junior Android developer in late 2026 to a senior-level AI photo and video generation practitioner and engineer by 2027—capable of producing studio-grade, identity-consistent photos and videos end-to-end across commercial APIs, custom local 128GB unified-memory setups, and rented cloud GPUs.

## Success looks like
- **Capstone 1 — Professional LinkedIn Photos:** Identity-locked, studio-lit headshots and environmental business portraits with realistic skin texture, natural catchlights, and zero "plastic AI" artifacts.
- **Capstone 2 — Tinder Dating Photos:** Candid, natural-light lifestyle, hobby, and full-body portraits across varied scenes that preserve exact facial and body proportions.
- **Capstone 3 — Cat Photos & Videos:** Custom pet LoRA + FLUX/SDXL keyframes + commercial (Veo 3.1 / Seedance 2.5) and open-weight (Wan 2.2 / HunyuanVideo) multi-shot cat videos with synchronized audio.
- **Capstone 4 (Optional) — Local Uncensored / NSFW Photos & Videos:** Private, offline-capable local ComfyUI workflows (SDXL/Pony/Illustrious, FLUX unlock LoRAs, local Wan I2V) with full technical mastery and strict ethical/consent boundaries.
- **Infrastructure Mastery:** Run ComfyUI + heavy open-weight models smoothly on both **M5 Max 128GB** and **ROG Flow Z13 AI Max+ 395 (128GB)**, and spin up **RunPod / Vast.ai** cloud GPUs when high-speed LoRA training (`ai-toolkit`, `kohya_ss`) beats local throughput.

## Constraints
- Current background: Junior Android developer (comfortable with code, JSON, APIs, CLI, and debugging; new to diffusion pipelines and GPU workflows).
- Local hardware: MacBook Pro M5 Max (128GB UMA) + ASUS ROG Flow Z13 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 (128GB UMA). Massive VRAM capacity, moderate memory bandwidth vs. discrete H100/RTX 5090—so use local 128GB for private inference/experimentation and cloud GPUs for rapid LoRA training.
- Subscriptions: Google AI Pro (plus willingness to add API credits/other plans where high leverage).
- Interactive learning style: short Tufte-style HTML lessons with code multiple-choice quizzes explaining why every answer is right or wrong.

## Out of scope
- Building a separate Android or Web SaaS application for the capstone (focus stays 100% on mastering photo/video generation, LoRA training, ComfyUI workflows, and producing the four visual capstones).
- Training foundational multi-billion-parameter diffusion models from scratch.
- Non-consensual imagery or deepfakes of other real people without explicit consent.
