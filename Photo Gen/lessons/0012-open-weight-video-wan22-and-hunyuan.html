2. How Wan 2.2 A14B Dual-Expert MoE Works in ComfyUI

Unlike single-model Wan 2.1, Wan 2.2 A14B splits denoising sequentially across two specialized 14B models sharing one latent flow:

  1. High-Noise Expert (Early Steps): Sculpted layout, big camera motion, and physics from step 0 → split.
  2. Low-Noise Expert (Late Steps): Refines fur detail, eyes, and color fidelity from step split → end.

Because each stage uses a separate DiT model, attach matched _high_noise.safetensors and _low_noise.safetensors LoRAs (including LightX2V/Lightning speed LoRAs) using LoraLoaderModelOnly (so it doesn't corrupt the shared umt5_xxl text encoder) and apply ModelSamplingSD3 (shift = 8.0)!

3. Spatiotemporal Latents (The 4k + 1 Frame Rule) & FLF2V

Valid Frame Counts: Because 3D video VAEs compress time 4× plus frame 0, always pick num_frames = 4k + 1 (e.g., 49, 65, 81 frames—never arbitrary counts like 50!). Video DiTs extend the 2D latent into a 5D spatiotemporal tensor [B, C, T_lat, H_lat, W_lat]. At 16–24 fps, 81 frames equals ~3.4–5.0 seconds of fluid video. You can also supply both a Start Image and an End Image via Wan 2.2 FLF2V (First-Last-Frame-to-Video) to choreograph exact camera transitions between two hero keyframes.

4. Speeding Up Video 4×–6× on M5 Max & ROG Z13

  • Distilled Step LoRAs (Game Changer on M5 Max!): Pair Wan 2.2 with matched high/low LightX2V or Wan-Lightning LoRAs to drop inference from 20–30 steps down to 4–6 total steps across the two samplers—while keeping BlockSwap OFF on 128GB UMA!
  • TeaCache: Caches redundant attention activations across adjacent denoising steps for 1.5×–2× speedups on ROCm / CUDA.
  • Preview Low, Upscale High: Iterate prompt & motion at 832×480 / 49 frames first, then render the winning seed at 720p + 81 frames + RIFE interpolation!

5. Hands-On Verification Lab

Render Your First Local Wan 2.2 Clip
  1. Open ComfyUI's built-in menu Workflow → Browse Templates → Wan 2.2 (or swap the loaders for UnetLoaderGGUF Q5_K_M on your M5 Max / FP8 on Z13).
