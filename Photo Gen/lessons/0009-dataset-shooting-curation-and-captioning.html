Dataset Capture, EXIF Normalization, Bucketing & Auto-Captioning

Garbage in, plastic AI out. Here is the exact shot-by-shot blueprint for your selfies, full-body photos, and cat photos/videos—followed by EXIF/HEIC cleanup, bucketing, and VLM-assisted captioning.

1. What Photos & Videos to Shoot Now

Dataset A: Your Own Selfies & Full-Body Photos (20–25 Photos)

Print Version: Keep Ref 02: Dataset Capture Manual handy on your phone while shooting. Quality and diversity beat quantity: 22 crisp, varied photos train a sharper FLUX/SDXL LoRA than 80 repetitive mirror selfies. Split them cleanly:

  • 8–10 Close-Ups (Head & Shoulders): Eye-level straight-on (confident neutral for LinkedIn + warm genuine smile with teeth), 30°–45° left/right turns, 2 true side profiles (teaches jawline & nose), and relaxed candid glances.
  • 6–7 Medium / 3/4 Shots (Waist-up): Standing & seated at a cafe/desk with hands naturally visible (teaches arm length, hand scale, and posture).
  • 5–6 Full-Body Shots (Head to Toe): Front standing, 45° stance, walking mid-stride, sitting on steps—never crop off ankles or shoes! Without full-body shots, LoRAs invent generic body ratios on Tinder lifestyle scenes.
  • What to Wear (Rotate 5–7 Outfits): Plain, well-fitting tees, a button-down shirt, sweater/jacket, and jeans/chinos. Never wear the same shirt in >3 photos (otherwise the model bakes that collar into your identity). Zero logos, sunglasses, or hats.
  • Camera & Lighting Rules: Use the rear 1x or 2x/50mm camera on a timer/tripod (never wide-angle arm's-length selfie distortion), turn OFF Portrait Mode & beauty smoothing, and shoot across 4–5 locations/lighting moods.

Dataset B: Your Cat Photos & Reference Videos (15–25 Photos + 3–5 Clips)

  • 6–8 Face Close-Ups (at cat eye-level!): Straight-on, 45°, and profile showing eye color, nose leather, whiskers, and forehead markings.
  • 6–8 Medium Shots: Loafing on couch, curled sleeping, sitting upright by a sunny window, perched on cat tree—mix sleepy half-closed eyes and wide-eyed alertness.
  • 5–7 Full-Body & Action Shots: Side-profile standing (teaches tail length, leg height, flank fur pattern), stretching, walking in bright daylight so paws aren't motion-blurred.
  • 3–5 Steady Reference Video Clips (3–5 sec, 1080p): Braced/tripod shots of (1) blinking/head-tilt, (2) walking side-to-side, (3) batting a wand toy—used directly as motion references in Seedance 2.5 and timing guides for Wan 2.2.

2. EXIF Orientation & HEIC Cleanup (Before Training!)

iPhone photos often arrive as .HEIC with an EXIF rotation tag—if a training dataloader ignores EXIF rotation, half your selfies load sideways! Always normalize to clean upright RGB .png or .jpg files first:

Python · Normalize EXIF Orientation & Verify Resolution
from pathlib import Path
from PIL import Image, ImageOps

src_dir = Path("datasets/me_raw_phone")
out_dir = Path("datasets/me_raw")
out_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)

for idx, p in enumerate(sorted(src_dir.glob("*")), start=1):
    if p.suffix.lower() not in {".jpg", ".jpeg", ".png", ".webp"}:
        continue
    img = ImageOps.exif_transpose(Image.open(p)).convert("RGB")
    assert min(img.size) >= 768, f"{p.name} too small ({img.size}); reshoot!"
    img.save(out_dir / f"me_{idx:02d}.png")

3. Aspect-Ratio Bucketing & VLM-Assisted Captioning

Do not square-crop every photo to 1024×1024. Modern trainers like ai-toolkit automatically group portrait (3:4), landscape (4:3), and square shots into ~1MP resolution buckets.

For captioning 20–25 photos fast: run a local VLM auto-captioner (e.g., JoyCaption or Qwen-VL) to generate base descriptions, then manually audit every .txt file using the Golden Caption Rule: start with your rare trigger token (ohwxman / mycatzuzu), describe everything variable (pose, outfit, lighting, environment), and delete permanent identity traits (your eye color, cat's tabby pattern) so the LoRA weights absorb them!

Text · Audited Paired Captions (datasets/me_raw/ & datasets/cat_raw/)
# datasets/me_raw/me_01.txt
ohwxman, full body shot standing casually on a sunlit park path, wearing a
plain olive crewneck t-shirt and dark jeans, relaxed smile, soft open shade.

# datasets/cat_raw/cat_04.txt
mycatzuzu, close-up eye-level portrait sitting on a wooden windowsill,
soft morning sunlight illuminating eyes, warm indoor background.

4. Hands-On Verification Lab

Assemble & Validate Your Dataset Folder
  1. Shoot your 20–25 selfies/full-body photos and 15–25 cat photos + 3–5 clips using Ref 02.
  2. Run the EXIF-normalization Python snippet above and verify every image in datasets/me_raw/ and datasets/cat_raw/ opens upright with min(width, height) ≥ 768px and zero Apple Portrait blur.
  3. Create paired .txt captions for all photos and verify you removed permanent traits (coat pattern, eye color, hairline) while keeping outfit/pose/lighting tags accurate.
