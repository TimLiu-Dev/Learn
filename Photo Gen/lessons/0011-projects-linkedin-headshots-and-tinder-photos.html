Capstones 1 & 2: Studio LinkedIn & Natural Tinder Photos

Turning your personal identity LoRA into indistinguishable, human-grade portraits—combining ControlNet Union pose/depth, exact FaceDetailer / HandDetailer graphs, outpainting, and anti-plastic color finishing.

1. Why Raw Single-Pass Generations Still Look "AI"

Even with a great identity LoRA, a single KSampler pass often leaves subtle giveaways: overly smooth "airbrushed" skin, soft eyes at full-body scale, or awkward finger anatomy in candid cafe shots. Senior portrait workflows solve this in three deliberate stages:

Stage 1: Base Composition
FLUX.2 / FLUX + Face LoRA (0.80) + model-matched ControlNet Depth/OpenPose (or PuLID anchor).
Stage 2: Face & Hand Detailer
UltralyticsDetectorProvider (face_yolov8m.pt / hand_yolov8s.pt) + SAMLoader → crop & re-denoise at denoise=0.35.
Stage 3: Grain & Outpaint
Extend tight crops via ImagePadForOutpaint + add subtle 35mm sensor grain so pores pass 200% zoom.

2. Recipe A — Professional LinkedIn Headshot (Capstone 1)

  • Framing & Lens: Chest-up portrait, 85mm f/2.0 look, subtle background compression.
  • Lighting & Outfit: Soft 45° key light + gentle fill (never flat ring-light glare), tailored charcoal or navy blazer, relaxed subtle smile showing approachable confidence.
  • ControlNet Compatibility Rule: Never plug an SDXL ControlNet into a FLUX workflow—model architectures and latent channels differ! Match FLUX.2 ControlNet Union to FLUX.2, or FLUX.1 ControlNet Union Pro to FLUX.1.

4. Hands-On Verification Lab (Ship Packs 1 & 2 Drafts)

Generate & Inspect Your LinkedIn + Tinder Drafts
  1. Load your trained Face LoRA at strength_model = 0.80 in ComfyUI on your M5 Max or Z13.
  2. Generate 3 LinkedIn headshots (varying blazer color & softbox/window angle) and 5 Tinder lifestyle photos across 35mm/50mm scenes (at least 2 full-body!).
  3. Pass the full-body Tinder shots through FaceDetailer (denoise=0.35) and check any hands with HandDetailer or manual masking (Lesson 07).
