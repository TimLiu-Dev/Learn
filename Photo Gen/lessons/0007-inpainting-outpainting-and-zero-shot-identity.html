Module 2 · Lesson 07 · Surgical Editing & Zero-Shot Control

Inpainting, Outpainting, Masking & Zero-Shot Identity Conditioning

Before training a custom LoRA, learn how senior engineers perform surgical edits—fixing warped hands, expanding tight crops into 3:4 Tinder frames, and locking identity or wardrobe zero-shot via FLUX.2 Multi-Reference, PuLID, and ControlNet Union.

1. Masking, Inpainting & Outpainting Mechanics

Android Analogy: Think of a ComfyUI MASK tensor as a grayscale alpha channel (0.0 = freeze original pixels, 1.0 = allow denoiser to repaint). Always feather/blur mask edges by 8–24px so seams disappear! Regenerating an entire image to fix one distorted finger or awkward coffee cup throws away a great composition. Instead, ComfyUI masks isolate updates strictly to target regions:

  • Inpainting (SetLatentNoiseMask / VAEEncodeForInpaint): Encodes the existing image into latent space, attaches your painted mask, and runs KSampler at denoise = 0.45–0.75 (higher denoise for replacing an object or mangled hand; lower 0.25–0.35 for subtle skin/eye cleanup).
  • Differential Diffusion (DifferentialDiffusion node): Replaces binary hard masks with smooth per-pixel denoise gradients—blending lighting and skin tone seamlessly across mask borders.
  • Outpainting (ImagePadForOutpaint): Pads canvas borders (e.g., turning a tight 1:1 headshot into a wider 3:4 waist-up portrait) with feathered edge masks so the model extends shoulders, outfit, and cafe background naturally.

2. Zero-Shot Identity & Multi-Reference Conditioning (Before LoRA!)

Sometimes you only have 1–3 reference photos (a friend, a specific blazer, or a props object) and don't want to spend 25 minutes training a LoRA. Late-2026 open-weight pipelines give you three zero-shot tools:

Method How It Works Best Use Case
FLUX.2 Native Multi-Reference Encodes up to 10 reference images as native visual context tokens inside the unified conditioning stream. Consistent outfits, props, room continuity across Cat Video shots, and rapid zero-shot identity tests.
PuLID for FLUX.2 / FLUX.1 Injects facial identity embeddings from an InsightFace/EVA-CLIP encoder into cross-attention layers with minimal style pollution. Quick zero-shot headshot drafts or pairing a custom LoRA with extra identity anchoring on distant shots.
ControlNet Union Orchestrator Loads one unified side-adapter supporting Depth, OpenPose, Canny, and Silhouette branches without multiplying VRAM. Locking exact shoulder posture and head tilt for LinkedIn & Tinder compositions.

3. Zero-Shot vs. Custom LoRA (When Each Wins)

  • Use Zero-Shot Multi-Ref / PuLID when: Testing lighting/poses fast, transferring an exact jacket/couch/prop, or rendering side characters you only have 1–2 photos of.
  • Train a Custom LoRA (Module 4) when: You need 95%+ likeness across extreme angles, expressions, full-body proportions, and your cat's asymmetric fur markings without reference-image pose stiffness.

4. Hands-On Verification Lab

Outpaint a Square Portrait & Fix a Detail
  1. Load one tight square portrait into ComfyUI, right-click → Open in MaskEditor (or wire ImagePadForOutpaint with bottom=256, feathering=24).
  2. Run VAEEncodeForInpaint + KSampler (denoise=0.65) and compare hard feathering=0 vs. feathering=24 + DifferentialDiffusion—verify the seam disappears completely.
