Capstone 3: Multi-Shot Cat Photos & Videos

Combining your custom Cat LoRA, FLUX/FLUX.2 keyframes, Wan 2.2 I2V & FLF2V, MMAudio SFX synthesis, RIFE interpolation, and ffmpeg finishing into a cohesive short film starring your own cat.

1. The Hybrid Keyframe-to-Video Architecture

Pure Text-to-Video (T2V) struggles to preserve unique pet markings (specific forehead "M" tabby stripes, asymmetrical socks, exact eye hue). The professional workflow separates identity/art direction from motion synthesis:

1. Hero Keyframes
Generate 3–4 storyboard shots using FLUX + Your Cat LoRA (mycatzuzu) + FLUX.2 Multi-Ref for room consistency.
2. I2V & FLF2V
Animate each keyframe via Wan 2.2 I2V / FLF2V (local 128GB) or Seedance 2.5 / Veo 3.1.
3. RIFE + Upscale
Run RealESRGAN/SeedVR2 video upscaling + RIFE optical-flow interpolation (16fps → 32/48fps).
4. MMAudio & FFmpeg
Synthesize room SFX/purrs via ComfyUI-MMAudio and assemble matched cuts with ffmpeg.

2. Designing a 3-Shot Cat Storyboard (12–15 Seconds Total)

  • Shot 1 (Establishing, 4s): mycatzuzu sitting on a sunlit windowsill blinking slowly while dust motes float—gentle 5% camera push-in.
  • Shot 2 (Action via FLF2V or Motion Ref, 4s): Medium side shot of mycatzuzu crouching and pouncing across a wooden floor toward a feathered wand toy (or use First-Last-Frame-to-Video between a crouching keyframe and a landing keyframe!).
  • Shot 3 (Payoff Close-Up, 4s): Tight low-angle portrait of mycatzuzu curling up contentedly on a blanket, head resting on paws.
Prompt Rule · I2V Motion Prompts Only Describe Change
# WRONG (Redundant & confuses I2V):
"An orange tabby cat with emerald eyes and white paws sitting on a wooden windowsill..."

# RIGHT (Keyframe already contains pixels; prompt ONLY directs motion & camera):
"Slow subtle camera push-in. The cat blinks lazily, twitches its left ear,
and turns its head slightly toward the sunny window as light dust motes drift."

3. Audio Synthesis (MMAudio) & ffmpeg Multi-Shot Assembly

Silent clips feel unfinished, while mismatched exposure between shots breaks immersion. In ComfyUI, pipe decoded video frames into ComfyUI-MMAudio with an audio prompt ("soft cat purring, gentle paws padding on hardwood floor, quiet sunny room ambience"), or finish color matching and concatenation in ffmpeg:

Bash · FFmpeg Color Match, Concat & Audio Muxing
# 1. List your normalized 1080p/32fps shots in shots.txt:
# file 'shot1_window_audio.mp4'
# file 'shot2_pounce_audio.mp4'
# file 'shot3_nap_audio.mp4'

# 2. Concatenate cleanly with mild warm-contrast unify + AAC audio:
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i shots.txt \
  -vf "eq=contrast=1.03:saturation=1.02,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -preset slow \
  -c:a aac -b:a 192k \
  cat_capstone_final.mp4

  2. Pick 3 storyboard keyframes, animate via Wan 2.2 I2V / FLF2V (or Seedance 2.5), interpolate to 32fps+ with RIFE, add synchronized audio via MMAudio, and assemble cat_capstone_final.mp4 with ffmpeg.
