1. What Hiring Managers Actually Look For in 2027

Anybody can type prompts into a consumer web app. Senior AI photo/video roles (AI Creative Technologist, Generative Media Engineer, ComfyUI Pipeline Engineer) hire for control, consistency, reproducibility, and cost-aware systems engineering:

  • Before / After & Ablation Proof: Show your dataset curation methodology, checkpoint step-comparison grids (step 500 vs 1500 vs 2500), and how ControlNet + FaceDetailer solved small-face softness.
  • Multi-Model & Licensing Judgment: Explain when to use managed APIs (Seedance 2.5 50-asset multi-ref, Veo 3.1, GPT-Image-2.6, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) vs. open-weight local/cloud graphs (FLUX.2, Wan 2.2 MoE, SDXL)—including Apache 2.0 vs. commercial licensing rules.
  • Hardware & Production Ops Literacy: Document real numbers—quantized GGUF/FP8 inference on 128GB unified memory, ~$1 RunPod L40S LoRA runs, WebSocket sweeps + ArcFace evals, and Network-Volume serverless webhook architectures.
