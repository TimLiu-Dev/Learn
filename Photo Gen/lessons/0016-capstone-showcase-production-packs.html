1. Public Career Portfolio Acceptance Criteria (Packs 1–3)

Before calling a deliverable "done," every output in your three portfolio packs must pass a senior creative-technologist QA audit:

Public Capstone Pack Minimum Deliverable Quality & Engineering Gate
1. LinkedIn Photos 3–5 hero portraits (studio navy/charcoal blazer + environmental office) Passes 200% zoom: natural eye catchlights, real skin pores, zero ear/collar morphing, verified ArcFace similarity (≥ 0.55–0.65+) & clean 2K resolution.
2. Tinder Dating Photos 5–6 varied lifestyle shots (cafe, golden hour walk, hobby, full-body candid) Mixed 35mm/50mm natural lighting, zero "studio plastic" vibe, accurate hands and full-body proportions, diverse outfits & locations.
3. Cat Photos & Videos 6 hero cat photos + 1 cohesive 12–15s multi-shot cat video (with audio) Exact coat markings preserved across cuts (FLUX + Cat LoRA keyframes → Wan 2.2 I2V/FLF2V or Seedance 2.5 / Veo 3.1), 32fps+ RIFE smoothness, MMAudio/native SFX, LUT-matched cuts.
