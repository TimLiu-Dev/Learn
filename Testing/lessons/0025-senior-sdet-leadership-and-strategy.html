Lesson 25: Senior SDET Leadership & Strategy

Senior Testing Engineers don't become a "QA bottleneck" manually signing off every ticket. They multiply engineering speed by building shared test harnesses, driving Risk-Based Testing, closing the loop with Shift-Right production telemetry, and writing crisp Test Strategy RFCs.

01 Risk-Based Testing Matrix (Impact × Likelihood)

Testing budgets are finite. A payment webhook bug has Impact 5 (revenue loss); a rarely visited "About App" version footer has Impact 1. Allocate Mutation Testing, Contract Tests, Maestro/Playwright E2E, and k6 load budgets strictly proportional to Risk Score!

02 1-Page Test Strategy RFC & Shift-Right Observability

Testing does not stop at merge: Senior SDETs pair Shift-Left PR gates with Shift-Right production telemetry—verifying OpenTelemetry trace spans across async boundaries, running lightweight synthetic checkout probes, and halting canary rollouts automatically when crash-free users or P95 SLOs dip.
