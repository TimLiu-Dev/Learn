PART 2 REFERENCE (MODULES 14–26)

Senior Testing Engineer (SDET) Cheat Sheet

Cross-platform E2E, distributed contracts, non-functional quality gates, AI evals, deflaking, and sharded CI/CD orchestration.

Contracts, Faults & Multi-Platform E2E

  • Testcontainers + Pact: Real ephemeral Docker dependencies + consumer-driven contracts catch staging schema breaks pre-merge.
  • Maestro vs Appium 2/3: Maestro YAML for fast smoke flows; Appium (POM/Screenplay) for cross-platform black-box & cloud device farms; Kaspresso/UIAutomator for OS dialogs.
  • Playwright: Role-based locators, auto-waiting, custom test.extend fixtures, toHaveScreenshot(), HAR/route interception, storageState auth reuse, and Trace Viewer debugging.

Non-Functional, AI Evals & CI Scale

  • A11y & Security: WCAG contrast, 48dp touch targets, Compose enableAccessibilityChecks(), TalkBack traversal; OWASP MASVS checks for IDOR/BOLA, deep links, and encrypted storage.
  • Perf & Load: Macrobenchmark P95 startup/frame jank + Grafana k6 P95 latency & error-rate CI gates.
  • AI Evals: Assert schema validity, tool-call invariants, prompt-injection resilience, grounding rubrics, and statistical pass@k thresholds.
  • CI Scale & Shift-Right: <1% flake SLO, ephemeral PR envs, Flank/Playwright sharding, 3-tier pipelines, and OpenTelemetry canary rollback gates.
