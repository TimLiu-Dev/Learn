# Two-Part Career Track & Full Curriculum Built

The learning track was structured into two resume-ready stages matching the user's requested career progression from Junior Android Developer:
1. **Part 1 (`0001`–`0013`):** Senior Android Developer quality mastery + **Project 1 ("PulseCart Android")** covering Black/White-Box design, Mutation Testing, DI testability, JUnit 5, MockK/Fakes, Coroutines/Turbine, TDD/Property Testing, Robolectric/Room, MockWebServer, Compose UI, and Roborazzi visual snapshots.
2. **Part 2 (`0014`–`0026`):** Senior Testing Engineer (SDET) mastery + **Capstone Project 2 ("ShopPulse E2E")** covering Testcontainers/Pact, Offline/Chaos, Espresso/UI Automator/Kaspresso, Appium 2/3 vs Maestro, Playwright, Accessibility, Macrobenchmark/k6, OWASP MASVS/BOLA, AI Evals, Deflaking, Sharded CI/CD, and Test Strategy Leadership.

- **Implications:** Future coaching sessions should track which Part/Module the learner is practicing and verify their lab/quiz retention before advancing.
