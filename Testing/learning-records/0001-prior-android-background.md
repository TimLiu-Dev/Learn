# Prior Android Development Baseline Disclosed

The learner starts from a Junior Android Developer background (Kotlin, Gradle, Android Studio, Activities/Compose, ViewModels/Repositories) targeting a Senior Testing Engineer (SDET) role in 2027.

- **Evidence:** Stated directly in initial prompt ("become a senior testing engineer from a junior android developer in 2027").
- **Implications:** Skip introductory Kotlin/OOP/Android lifecycle explanations; ground testing concepts immediately in ViewModels, Compose, Coroutines, Retrofit/Room, and Gradle `src/test` vs `src/androidTest`, while explicitly teaching cross-stack SDET areas outside native Android (Appium, Playwright, Testcontainers, Pact, k6, OWASP, CI orchestration).
