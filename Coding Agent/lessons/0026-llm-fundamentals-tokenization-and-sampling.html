LLM Internals: BPE Tokenization, Context Budgets & Sampling Controls

Coming from Android and Kotlin, you already think in memory budgets, UTF-16 strings, and deterministic APIs. LLM systems instead operate over subword tokens, finite context windows, and temperature-scaled probability distributions. Mastering these mechanics eliminates silent truncation bugs and unstable agent loops.

1. AI Engineer vs. ML Researcher & The 2027 Model Ecosystem

As an AI Engineer, your job is building reliable software systems atop pre-trained foundation models rather than training weights from scratch. Production architectures combine two complementary families:

  • Frontier Managed APIs: Closed models optimized for deep reasoning and tool use (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT / o-series, Google Gemini). Higher per-token cost, zero GPU ops overhead.
  • Open-Weights Ecosystems: Self-hostable checkpoints via Hugging Face, Ollama, or vLLM (Meta Llama, Qwen-Coder, DeepSeek, Mistral). Full data residency inside VPCs or edge devices.

2. Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) & Why Code Eats Tokens

Transformers do not read characters or fixed words; they operate on subword tokens produced by Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE). Common English words ("return", "function") encode as 1 token (~4 characters), whereas dense Kotlin punctuation, UUIDs, indented whitespace, or camelCase identifiers fracture into many tokens (~2–2.5 chars/token). Counting characters instead of tokens causes sudden HTTP 400 context_length_exceeded crashes.

3. Temperature, Top-p Nucleus Sampling & Reasoning Tradeoffs

  • temperature (T): Scales raw logits via z_i / T before softmax. Low temperature (0.0–0.2) sharpens probability onto the top candidate for deterministic JSON tool schemas and bug patches; high temperature (0.7+) flattens the curve for brainstorming or MCTS candidate branching.
  • top_p (Nucleus Sampling): Sorts tokens by descending probability and keeps the smallest prefix whose cumulative probability meets or exceeds top_p (e.g., 0.9), truncating incoherent long-tail noise regardless of flat distributions.
  • Knowledge Cutoffs & Grounding: Static weights freeze at training time; never rely on parametric memory for new Android SDKs or internal APIs without Retrieval (RAG) or tool lookup.
